Now let's see what happens when $T \to 0$. Consider a

ratio like

$(m \ne 0)$

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{P_{0,i}}{P_m} &= e^{-\beta(E_0-E_m)}
\end{aligned}$$

Since $E_0 < E_m$ the quantity $-\beta(E_0-E_m)$ is positive. And when $T \to 0$ it becomes very large. Thus $e^{-\beta(E_0-E_m)}$ will be a really really big number, which implies

$$\begin{aligned}
P_{0,i} &\gg P_m
\end{aligned}\tag{16}$$
