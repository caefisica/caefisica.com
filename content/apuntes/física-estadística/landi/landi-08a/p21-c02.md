where $V(y_A,y_B)$ is the interaction energy. We will assume that this interaction is weak

$$\begin{aligned}
V &\ll H_{A,B}
\end{aligned}\tag{31}$$

which is known as the weak coupling approximation. This is usually true in most systems since $H_{A,B}$ are volumetric quantities, whereas $V$ depends on the surface of the systems (the number of atoms in the surface is much smaller than on the bulk).

Let $y=(y_A,y_B)$ and denote by $y_t$ the microstate at time $t$. At $t=0$ the systems were coupled to heat baths so $y_0$ is a random variable with probability

$$\begin{aligned}
P(y_0) &= \frac{e^{-\beta_A H_A(y_A^0)}}{Z_A}\frac{e^{-\beta_B H_B(y_B^0)}}{Z_B}
\end{aligned}\tag{32}$$
