Let $y_A$ denote the set of microstates of system A and  
similarly for $y_B$ and system B. For $t>0$ we can descri-  
be the total energy of A+B as

$$\begin{aligned}
H(y_A,y_B) &= H_A(y_A) + H_B(y_B) + V(y_A,y_B)
\end{aligned}\tag{30}$$
