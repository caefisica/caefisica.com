Then $\partial F'/\partial\alpha=0$ gives

$\displaystyle \frac{\partial F'}{\partial\alpha}=\sum_m P_m-1=0$

which is the normalization. The value of $\alpha$ is fixed
in the end to ensure that $\displaystyle \sum_m P_m=1$.

Differentiating $F'$ with respect to $P_m$ we get

$\displaystyle \frac{\partial F'}{\partial P_m}=E_m+k_BT\ln P_m+k_BT+\alpha=0$

Thus

$\displaystyle \ln P_m=-\frac{E_m}{k_BT}-\frac{\alpha}{k_BT}-1$

or

$\displaystyle P_m=e^{-\beta E_m}e^{-\beta\alpha-1}$
