Then

$c = \frac{\partial F}{\partial T} + S + T\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}$

But $\partial F/\partial T = -S$ so the first two terms cancel and

$$\begin{aligned}
c &= T\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}
\end{aligned}\tag{12}$$

this is how people measure the entropy of a system:

$$\begin{aligned}
S &= \int \frac{c}{T}\,dT + \text{const}
\end{aligned}\tag{13}$$

they integrate $c/T$. Note also that entropy can only be measured up to a constant.
