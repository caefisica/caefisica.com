or, what is even better

$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta Q &= T\Delta S
\end{aligned}\tag{29}$$

This establishes a famous relation in thermodynamics, between heat and entropy changes. We will learn later that this continues to be true when work is performed in the system.

Eq (29) can also be used to define an adiabatic process; i.e., a process for which no heat flows through the system. We see, in terms of (29), that this is a synonym of a process which occurs at constant entropy.
