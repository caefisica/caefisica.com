But there are some materials, like the spin glasses,  
where the degeneracy scales like $g \sim \lambda^\nu$, for some  
constant $\lambda$. Thus

$$\frac{S}{\nu} = \frac{k_B}{\nu}\ln \lambda^\nu = k_B \ln \lambda = \text{finite}$$
