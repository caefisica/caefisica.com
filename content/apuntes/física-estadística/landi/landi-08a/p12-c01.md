what I want to show is that the distribution

$P_m$ which minimizes $F$ is exactly the Gibbs distribu-
tion.

To do that we need to minimize $F$ with respect
to each $P_m$. But this minimization must be subject to
the constraint that $\sum_m P_m = 1$. To do that we intro-
duce a Lagrange multiplier

$$\begin{aligned}
F' &= \sum_m \left\{F_mP_m + k_BT P_m \ln P_m\right\} + \alpha\left(\sum_m P_m -1\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{23}$$
