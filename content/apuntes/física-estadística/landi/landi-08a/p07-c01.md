The limit $T \to 0$

Now I want to understand what happens with $P_m, V, S, F, C, \ldots$ in the limit $T \to 0$. In this case we must be careful to consider the degeneracy of the ground state.

Suppose that the ground state has degeneracy $g$ (same integer, like 42). this means there are $g$ states with energy $E_0$. Let us label them by $i = 1, \ldots, g$.

we then write

$$\begin{aligned}
E_0 = E_{0,i} < E_1 \leq E_2 \leq E_3 \cdots
\end{aligned}\tag{15}$$
