$v\ne0$ the spins are no longer independent and we must use

$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sigma_i\right)
&=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\operatorname{Var}(\sigma_i)
+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i\ne j}\operatorname{Cov}(\sigma_i,\sigma_j)
\end{aligned}\tag{58}$$
