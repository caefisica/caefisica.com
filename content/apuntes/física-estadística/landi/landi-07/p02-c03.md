Ex.: a spin-$1/2$ system has quantum number $S=+\frac{1}{2}$ or  
$\sigma=-\frac{1}{2}$, the energy in the presence of a magnetic  
field $B$ is $E_\sigma=-\mu_B B\sigma$, where $\mu_B$ is a constant.

Ex.: A quantum harmonic oscillator has a quantum  
number

$m=0,1,2,3,\ldots$

with energy levels

$$\begin{aligned}
E_m &= \hbar\omega\left(m+\frac{1}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
