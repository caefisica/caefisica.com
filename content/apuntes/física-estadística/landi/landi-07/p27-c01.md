Now comes a point I really want you to remember. If $v=0$ (no interactions) then the $\sigma_i$ will be statistically independent. In this case $\operatorname{Var}(x_1+\cdots+x_N)=\operatorname{Var}(x_1)+\cdots+\operatorname{Var}(x_N)$.

Thus we get

$$\begin{aligned}
\chi &= \frac{\mu_B^2}{k_B T}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[\langle\sigma_i^2\rangle-\langle\sigma_i\rangle^2\right]
\end{aligned}\tag{57}$$
