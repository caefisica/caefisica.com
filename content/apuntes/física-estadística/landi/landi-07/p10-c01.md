The partition function is

$$\begin{aligned}
Z &= \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} e^{-\beta\hbar\omega(m+1/2)}
= e^{-\beta\hbar\omega/2}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}e^{-\beta\hbar\omega m}
= e^{-\beta\hbar\omega/2}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}x^m
\end{aligned}$$

$x=e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}$

This is a geometric series

$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}x^m=\frac{1}{1-x}
\end{aligned}\tag{19}$$
