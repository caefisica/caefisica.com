$ \frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1} := N = "\langle m\rangle" $ \tag{3.1}

I write "$\langle m\rangle$" because this is not the actual $\langle m\rangle$ of the molecule. It is the $\langle m\rangle$ the molecule would have in thermal equilibrium.

Note also that

$$\begin{aligned}
N+1 &= \frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}+1
= \frac{1+e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
= \frac{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
\end{aligned}\tag{3.2}$$
