Specific heat and the Einstein model

The heat capacity will be from (2.3)

$$\begin{aligned}
c &= \frac{\partial U}{\partial T}
= -\frac{1}{k_B T^2}\frac{\partial U}{\partial \beta}
= -\frac{1}{k_B T^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial \beta}
\left[\frac{\hbar\omega}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}\right]
\end{aligned}$$
