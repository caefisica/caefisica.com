Quantum systems

Now we will extend the Gibbs formalism to include  
quantum systems. the big difference is that in quantum  
systems the energies take on a discrete set of values

$ \text{Energy} = E_1 \text{ or } E_2 \text{ or } E_3 \text{ or } E_4 \text{ or } \cdots $
