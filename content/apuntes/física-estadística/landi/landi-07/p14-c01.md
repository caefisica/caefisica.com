At $T=0$ we see that $U$ heads to the ground state, $U=E_0=\frac{\hbar\omega}{2}$,

Let's see what happens in the opposite limit, at high temperatures.

In this case $\frac{\hbar\omega}{k_BT}<1$ so we may expand coth$(x)$. It's first

Taylor series coefficient in

$\coth(x)\simeq\frac{1}{x}$

Thus, Eq (23) will give

$$\begin{aligned}
U &\simeq \frac{\hbar\omega}{2}\frac{2k_BT}{\hbar\omega}=k_BT
\end{aligned}\tag{24}$$
