Susceptibility of a wave general spin system

Consider now a system containing $N$ spin $1/2$ particles. Each is described by a variable $\sigma_i = \pm 1$. we assume the energy is

$$\begin{aligned}
E(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N) = -\mu_B B \sum_{i=1}^{N}\sigma_i + N(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N)
\end{aligned}\tag{50}$$
