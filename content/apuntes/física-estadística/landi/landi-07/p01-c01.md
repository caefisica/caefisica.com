Gibbs formalism for quantum systems

Gabriel T. Lawdi

Summary of main formulas

classical                                             quantum

$P(y)=\frac{e^{-\beta H(y)}}{Z}$                 $P_m=\frac{e^{-\beta E_m}}{Z}$

$Z=\int \mathcal{D}y\,e^{-\beta H(y)}$         $Z=\sum_m e^{-\beta E_m}$

$U=\langle H\rangle=\int H(y)P(y)\mathcal{D}y$   $U=\langle E_m\rangle=\sum_m E_mP_m$

$C=\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}$

$F=-k_BT\ln Z$
