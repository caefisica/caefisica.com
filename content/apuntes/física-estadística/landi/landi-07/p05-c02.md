$$\begin{aligned}
z&=\sum_{m=0,1}e^{-\beta E_m}=e^{-\beta E_0}+e^{-\beta E_1}\\
\therefore\quad z&=1+e^{-\beta\epsilon}
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

The probabilities are then $P_m=\dfrac{e^{-\beta E_m}}{z}$, or

$$\begin{aligned}
P_0&=\frac{1}{1+e^{-\beta\epsilon}}\\
P_1&=\frac{e^{-\beta\epsilon}}{1+e^{-\beta\epsilon}}=\frac{1}{e^{\beta\epsilon}+1}
\end{aligned}\tag{8}$$

$P_1$ is known as the Fermi-Dirac distribution. It gives you the probability of finding the system in the excited state;
