On the other hand, we may work this out explicitly.

$\chi = -\frac{\partial^2}{\partial B^2}\left[-k_B T \ln z\right] = k_B T\left[\frac{z''}{z} - \left(\frac{z'}{z}\right)^2\right]$

where $z' = \frac{\partial z}{\partial B}$. Now;

$\frac{z'}{z} = \beta \langle\mu\rangle$

and

$\frac{z''}{z} = \beta^2 \langle\mu^2\rangle$
