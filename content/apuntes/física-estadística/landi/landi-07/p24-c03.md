If the spins interact this cannot be factored as a product of independent terms. we will see an example of this later on.

Now, what we want is the magnetization, defined as

$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \langle \mu \rangle_L \qquad M = \mu_B \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sigma_i
\end{aligned}\tag{52}$$

so notice that we may write the energy (50) as

$$\begin{aligned}
E(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N) = -BM + V
\end{aligned}\tag{53}$$
