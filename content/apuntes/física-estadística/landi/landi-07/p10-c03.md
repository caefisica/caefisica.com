The Gibbs probabilities are then

$$P_m=\frac{e^{-\beta E_m}}{Z}=\frac{1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}}{e^{-\beta\hbar\omega/2}}e^{-\beta\hbar\omega(m+1/2)}$$

Notice how the factor of $\beta\hbar\omega/2$ will cancel out- this is reasonable.

After all, a constant shift in all the energy levels should not affect the probabilities. we then get

$$\begin{aligned}
P_m&=(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})e^{-\beta\hbar\omega m}
\end{aligned}\tag{2}$$

this is the Geometric distribution $P_m=(1-p)^m p$, with $p=1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}$
