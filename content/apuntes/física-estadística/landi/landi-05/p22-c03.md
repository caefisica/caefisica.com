$1\;-\;2\;-\;3\;-\;4\;-\;5\;-\;6$

The degree of the nodes are no longer all equal.

$$\begin{aligned}
d_m&=\begin{cases}
2,&\text{if }m=2,\ldots,5\\
1,&\text{if }m=1\text{ or }6
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\tag{34}$$

Thus $\sum_m d_m=1+4\times2+1=10$ and the steady-state proba-  
bilities will be

$$\begin{aligned}
P_m&=\begin{cases}
\frac{2}{10},&m=2,3,4,5\\
\frac{1}{10},&m=1\text{ or }6
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\tag{35}$$

The prob. of finding the system at the boundaries is smaller,  
the ring is what we call periodic boundary conditions (PBC)  
and the line is called open boundary conditions (ABC). PBC  
has translational invariance but ABC does not.
