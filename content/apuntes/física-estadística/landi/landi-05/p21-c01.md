Continuing with (20), we see that:

$$\begin{aligned}
P_m' &= d_m
\end{aligned}\tag{31}$$

But this probability is not yet normalized, so the actual  
steady-state distribution will be

$$\begin{aligned}
P_m &= \frac{d_m}{\sum_m d_m}
\end{aligned}\tag{32}$$
