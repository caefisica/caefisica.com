Stochastic matrices

the transition matrix $Q$ satisfies some very special properties,  
First, its entries are always non-negative, this is obvious from  
the definition (3) (conditional probabilities are also probabilities!)

$$\begin{aligned}
Q_{m,m} = P(x_t = m \mid x_{t-1} = m) \geq 0
\end{aligned}\tag{15}$$
