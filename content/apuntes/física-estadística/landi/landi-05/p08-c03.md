$(x_1,p_1)\qquad (x_2,p_2)\qquad (x_3,p_3)\qquad (x_4,p_4)\qquad (x_5,p_5)\qquad (x_6,p_6)\qquad (x_7,p_7)$

There is, however, one big difference : at each node of the graph, there is only one edge coming in and one edge going out. If you are at $(x_i,p_i)$ then, with 100% certainty, you come from $(x_{i-1},p_{i-1})$ and with 100% certainty you will go to $(x_{i+1},p_{i+1})$. Newton's law is a deterministic Markov chain (trajectories in phase space never cross each other).
