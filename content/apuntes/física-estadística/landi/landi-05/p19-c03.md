We then get

$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_m Q_{nmm}p_m' &= \sum_m S_{nm}p_m'
\end{aligned}\tag{27}$$

This is a type of global balance relation. It says that the steady-state is that state for which all transitions are balanced out.

Some systems, particularly those that appear in physics, satisfy a stronger statement called detailed balance. Namely, not only the sum on the left and on the right are equal, but also its individual entries

$$\begin{aligned}
Q_{nmm}p_m' &= Q_{mmn}p_n'
\end{aligned}\tag{28}$$
