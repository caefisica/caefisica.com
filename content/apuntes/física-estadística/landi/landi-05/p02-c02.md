But the random walk is somewhat limited: since you always  
remain independent r.v.'s, the steps are always independent of the  
current state.

the Markov chain takes this a step further. It allows you to  
choose the next step based on information about your current  
state.

We will discuss here only the cases of discrete time and discrete  
space. this means that we will assume $X_t$ takes on discrete values  
and that $t$ changes in integer steps.

As the system evolves in time, $X_t$ will take on a series of  
random positions. this is called a stochastic trajectory
