For our example above $d_1 = 2, d_2 = 2, d_3 = 3, d_4 = 1$.

The transition probability $Q_{mn}$ in this case is

$$\boxed{Q_{mn} = \frac{1}{d_m}}\tag{2}$$

by the above arguments. we then see that

$$\boxed{Q_{mn}d_m = Q_{nm}d_n}\tag{3}$$

Thus, the random walk on a graph satisfies detailed balance  
[Eq (20)].
