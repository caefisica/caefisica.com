the matrix $Q$ is called the transition matrix

If we start at $t=0$ with a distribution $\vec{p}(0)$, then we obtain

$$\begin{aligned}
\vec{p}(1)&\infty \\
\vec{p}(1)&=Q\vec{p}(0)
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
