'A chain is irreducible if for each $(m,n)$ there exists  
an integer $j$ such that $(Q^j)_{mn}>0."

This means that, in $j$ steps it becomes possible to reach $n$  
starting from $m$.

Most theorems concerning the steady-state refer to  
irreducible matrices.
