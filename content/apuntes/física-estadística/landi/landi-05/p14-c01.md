Irreducible chain

A chain is called irreducible if it is possible to go  
from anywhere to anywhere.

(A) and (D) are irreducible but (B) and (C) are not.  
If you add a link $B \to C$ in (B), it will still not  
be irreducible because, if you start in the lower chain  
you still cannot reach the upper part.
