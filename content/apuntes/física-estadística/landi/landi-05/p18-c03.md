$$\begin{aligned}
\lim_{t\to\infty} Q^t\vec{P}(0) &= c_1\vec{z}_1
\end{aligned}\tag{26}$$

All other terms will vanish. Since $\vec{z}_1 \propto \vec{p}^*$ we now see why $Q^t\vec{P}(0)$ will converge to the steady-state (the constant $c_1$ is just a normalization constant, so we choose it in a way that exactly normalizes $c_1\vec{z}_1$).

Conclusion: if $Q$ has only one eigenvalue $1$ and all others with $|\lambda|<1$, then the chain will converge to the steady-state cyclic matrices are problematic because they have other eigenvalues with $|\lambda|=1$ so the other terms in (25) never get smaller. For instance, the eigenvalues of (29) are $1$, $(-1)^{2/3}$, $(-1)^{4/3}$.
