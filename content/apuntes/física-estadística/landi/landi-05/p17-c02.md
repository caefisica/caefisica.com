According to $E_q$ (21), if a chain is irreducible and regular (most cycles), we may compute its steady-state by starting from an arbitrary vector $\vec{p}(0)$ and simply applying $Q$ to it many times. Now I want to show you why this works.

Let us write the eigenvalue/eigenvector $E_q$ for $Q$ as

$$\begin{aligned}
Q\vec{x}_i &= \lambda_i\vec{x}_i
\end{aligned}\tag{22}$$
