Thus, the transition prob

$$\begin{aligned}
P_1(t+1) &= 0.8P_1(t)+0.4P_2(t)\\
P_2(t+1) &= 0.2P_1(t)+0.6P_2(t)
\end{aligned}\tag{11}$$

From this we immediately read off the transition matrix

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{Q=\begin{bmatrix}0.8&0.4\\0.2&0.6\end{bmatrix}}
\end{aligned}\tag{12}$$

Suppose that today ($t=0$) we knew its sunny. This means

that

$$\begin{aligned}
\vec{P}(0)=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}\tag{13a}$$

Then the prob. distribution for tomorrow will be

$$\begin{aligned}
\vec{P}(1)&=Q\vec{P}(0)=\begin{bmatrix}0.8&0.4\\0.2&0.6\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}0.8\\0.2\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}\tag{13b}$$
