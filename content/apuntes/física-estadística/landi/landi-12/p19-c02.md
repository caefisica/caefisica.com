The correlation between two spins very far apart therefore tends to $\cos^2\theta$. It is convenient to subtract this part and define the **connected correlation**

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned}
G(r) &= \langle \sigma_n \sigma_{n+r}\rangle - \langle \sigma_n^z\rangle\langle \sigma_{n+r}^z\rangle = \left(\frac{\lambda_-}{\lambda_+}\right)^r \sin^2\theta
\end{aligned}}\tag{3.34}$$

I wrote $G(r)$ and not $G(n,n+r)$ to emphasize that, due to translation symmetry, this result depends only on the distance between the spins. When $h \to 0$ this result reduces to Eq. (3.7).
