3.3 Non-zero field and the transfer matrix

Next we turn to the 1D Ising model when $h\ne 0$. In this case it is convenient to use periodic boundary conditions so we will write the energy as

$$\begin{aligned}
E&=-J\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sigma_n\sigma_{n+1}-h\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sigma_n
\end{aligned}\tag{3.10}$$

and the partition function is

$$\begin{aligned}
Z&=\sum_{\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N}e^{-\beta E}
=\sum_{\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N}\exp\left\{\beta J\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sigma_n\sigma_{n+1}+\beta h\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sigma_n\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{3.11}$$

This sum may be computed using a technique known as the transfer matrix, which can also be used in other models, in particular in the 2D Ising model.
