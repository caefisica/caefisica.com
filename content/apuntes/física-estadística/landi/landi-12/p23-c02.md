Analysis for $h = 0$

When $h = 0$ Eq. (3.41) becomes

$$\begin{aligned}
m &= \tanh(\beta 2Jmd)
\end{aligned}\tag{3.42}$$

This equation can be solved graphically. We have two functions, $m$ and $\tanh(\beta 2Jmd)$ and we must find the point where they coincide. The idea is illustrated in Fig. 3.12 for three possible values of $2\beta Jd$.

(b) $2\beta Jd = 1$
