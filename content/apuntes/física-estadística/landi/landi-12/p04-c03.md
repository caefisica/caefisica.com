3.2 The 1D Ising model: zero magnetic field

The one-dimension Ising model, which was the one actually studied by Ising in his PhD, is defined by a one-dimensional lattice with $N$ sites, each being represented by a Pauli matrix $\sigma_i^z$ (see Fig. 3.3). The Hamiltonian is taken to be

$$\begin{aligned}
H &= -J \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} \sigma_i^z \sigma_{i+1}^z
\end{aligned}\tag{3.1}$$
