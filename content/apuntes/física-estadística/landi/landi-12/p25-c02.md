The two-point correlation function is

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle \sigma_n \sigma_{n+r}\rangle &= \langle (m+\delta\sigma_n)(m+\delta\sigma_{n+r})\rangle
\end{aligned}\tag{3.48}$$

But, by definition, $\langle\delta\sigma_n\rangle = 0$. Moreover, since we are discarding quadratic terms in the fluctuations, we are left only with

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle \sigma_n \sigma_{n+r}\rangle &= m^2
\end{aligned}\tag{3.49}$$

The correlation function is independent of distance and is always the magnetization squared.
