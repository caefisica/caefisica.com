$$S\simeq k_B\ln 3\tag{49c}$$

The energy $U$ tends to the arithmetic average of all energies, since

all states are equally likely

$$\begin{aligned}
U&=\sum_m E_mP_m=E_0P_0+E_1P_1+E_2P_2\\
&=-\frac{2\mu H}{3}+0+0
\end{aligned}$$

the entropy tends to $k_B\ln 3$. Since all states are equally accessible, what enters here is the ln of the total number of states. Finally, $F=U-TS$. Since $U$ and $S$ are finite, but

$T\to\infty$, $F$ will tend to $-\infty$.
