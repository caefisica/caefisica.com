Summing these contributions for the entire process, we  
then find that the total work performed was

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{W} = \sum_{t=0}^{b-1} \left[ E(x_t,\lambda_{t+1}) - E(x_t,\lambda_t) \right]
\end{aligned}\tag{57}$$

whereas the total heat exchanged was

$$\begin{aligned}
Q = \sum_{t=0}^{b-1} \left[ E(x_{t+1},\lambda_{t+1}) - E(x_t,\lambda_{t+1}) \right]
\end{aligned}\tag{58}$$

If we add the two we see that most terms will cancel  
out. We will only be left with

$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta U = Q + W = E(x_b,\lambda_b) - E(x_0,\lambda_0)
\end{aligned}\tag{59}$$

this shows clearly why U is a function of state and Q,W are  
not.
