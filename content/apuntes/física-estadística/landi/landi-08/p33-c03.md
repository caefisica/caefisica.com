$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{P(\omega)}{P_R(-\omega)} &= e^{\beta(\omega-\Delta F)}
\end{aligned}\tag{68}$$

This is known as the Crooks fluctuation theorem (not to be confused with the fluctuation-dissipation theorem, which is something else).
