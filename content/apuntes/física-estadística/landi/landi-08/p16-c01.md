This formula puts on more concrete grounds the connection between heat capacity and heat and entropy.

This is also how one computes the entropy experimentally.

No one can measure entropy directly. what they do is they measure C and then integrate:

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial S}{\partial T} &= \frac{C}{T} \rightsquigarrow S = \int \frac{C}{T}\,dT
\end{aligned}\tag{35}$$
