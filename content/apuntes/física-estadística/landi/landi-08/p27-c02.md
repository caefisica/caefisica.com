Then an external agent begins to change $\lambda$ (ie, perform work).  
we will assume that $\lambda$ changes also in discrete steps and instantan-  
taneously. So at $t=0$ we change $\lambda_0 \to \lambda_1$ and then allow the  
system to evolve up to time $t=1$. then at $t=1$ we change  
(instantaneously) $\lambda_1 \to \lambda_2$ and then let the system evolve  
up to $t=2$. we assume this continues up to a time $t_0$.  
we will thus have a Markov chain  

$$\begin{aligned}
X_0 &\xrightarrow{\lambda_1} X_1 \xrightarrow{\lambda_2} X_2 \to \cdots \to X_{t_0-1} \xrightarrow{\lambda_{t_0}} X_{t_0}
\end{aligned}\tag{51}$$
