Thus we conclude that

$$\begin{aligned}
S &= \frac{U-F}{T} \quad \text{or} \quad F = U-TS
\end{aligned}\tag{26}$$

This is a well known relation from thermodynamics. It also gives a very practical way of computing the entropy. since F and U are easy to find.

Another useful formula is

$$\begin{aligned}
S &= -\frac{\partial F}{\partial T}
\end{aligned}\tag{27}$$
