Since work fluctuates, in a given experiment there is  
a certain probability to observe stochastic realizations  
where $w < \Delta F$, thus would therefore constitute $because$  
violations of the $second$ $law$.

the prob. of observing a violation in

$$\begin{aligned}
P(w < \Delta F) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\Delta F} P(w)\,dw
\end{aligned}\tag{73}$$
