At each step, when we change $\lambda$ abruptly from $\lambda_t$ to $\lambda_{t+1}$, we perform some work. Since the change is instantaneous the state of the system has not yet changed, so the work performed in this step will be

$$\begin{aligned}
\delta w_t = E(x_t,\lambda_{t+1}) - E(x_t,\lambda_t).
\end{aligned}\tag{55}$$
