$H>0$: $\beta\mu H \gg 1$ at low $T$.

So we may approximate $2+e^{\beta\mu H}\approx e^{\beta\mu H}$. We then get

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{F}{N} &\approx -k_BT\ln e^{\beta\mu H}=-\mu H
\end{aligned}\tag{46a}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{U}{N} &\approx -\mu H\frac{e^{\beta\mu H}}{e^{\beta\mu H}}=-\mu H
\end{aligned}\tag{46b}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{S}{Nk_B} &\approx -\beta\mu H\frac{e^{\beta\mu H}}{e^{\beta\mu H}}+\ln e^{\beta\mu H}=0
\end{aligned}\tag{46c}$$

when $H>0$ the ground state has energy $E_0=-\mu H$ and is non-degenerate. Thus $S\to 0$ and $U$ and $F$ tend to $E_0$.
