If the $\lambda$'s change very little and very slowly, the system will always remain close to equilibrium at all times. This is what we call a quasi-static process. But if $\lambda$ changes quickly the probabilities $P_m(t)$ will differ substantially from the probabilities $P_m^{eq}(\lambda,t)$.
