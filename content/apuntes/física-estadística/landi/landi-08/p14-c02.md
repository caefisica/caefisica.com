But if the ground state is degenerate, then we will have several states with the same energy. Let $P_{0,i}$ be the probabilities of these states and let $g$ denote the degeneracy of the ground state (so $i=1,\ldots,g$). Then all ground state will have the same probabilities which, by normalization must be

$$\begin{aligned}
P_{0,i} &= 1/g,\quad P_m=0\quad m>0
\end{aligned}\tag{29}$$
