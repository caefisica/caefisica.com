Comparison of Harmonic quantum theories

2-state system                 Quantum harm. oscillator

$E_0 = 0,\quad E_1 = \epsilon$         $E_m = \hbar\omega(m+1/2),\quad m=0,1,2,\ldots$

$Z = 1 + e^{-\beta\epsilon}$         $Z = \frac{e^{-\beta\hbar\omega/2}}{1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}}$

$U = \frac{\epsilon}{e^{\beta\epsilon}+1}$         $U = \frac{\hbar\omega}{2} + \frac{\hbar\omega}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}$

$F = -k_BT\ln(1+e^{-\beta\epsilon})$         $F = \frac{\hbar\omega}{2} + k_BT\ln(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})$
