This is the distribution of the full stochastic path. For systems satisfying the Markov property this probability is easy to find

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x_0=m_0,\ldots,x_b=m_b)&=Q_{m_b,m_{b-1}}(x_b)\cdots Q_{m_1,m_0}(x_1)p_{m_0}(x_0)
\end{aligned}\tag{60}$$
