The Jarzynski equality

Now I want to show you a remarkable consequence of Eq (68). Since we have $P(\omega)$ we may compute any quantity we want. For instance

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle \omega\rangle &= \int \omega P(\omega)\,d\omega
\end{aligned}\tag{69}$$
