In the first step the system is always at the same $T$  
(because you apply $B$ slowly). Since $T$ is forced and $B$ goes  
up, $U$ goes down [Eq (39)]. So in the end of (1) the temperature  
remained the same, but $U$ went down.

In step 2 you turn off $B$ very quickly, so there is no  
time to exchange heat. thus, $S$ remains constant (adiabatic;  
see figure). But if you look at $S$ in Eq (40) you will see that it  
depends only on $B/T$. Hence, if $S$ is constant but $B$ goes down,  
then so must $T$, hence cooling down your sample.
