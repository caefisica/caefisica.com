Entropy and heat capacity

Consider now a different experiment; a system is in  
equilibrium at a temperature $T$. then we get it and place  
it in contact with another bath, at a temperature $T+dT$  
[This is like taking a hot sword from the oven and dipping  
it in a bucket of water].

the change in the energy of the system will be

$$\begin{aligned}
dU &= \sum_m E_m\,dP_m
\end{aligned}\tag{31}$$

where, now,

$dP_m = P_m(T+dT) - P_m(T)$
