this function looks like this

$-x\ln x$

It is zero when $x=0$ and when $x=1$. Thus, any state that

has $P_m=0$ or $P_m=1$ will not contribute to the entropy.

This is why we say entropy is related to disorder. It's main

contributions come from states with moderate probabili-

ties.
