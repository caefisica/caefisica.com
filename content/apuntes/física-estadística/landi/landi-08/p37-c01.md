Thus, we see that from the Jarzynski equality we recover  
the $2^{nd}$ law (4.91) for the average work. For macroscopic  
systems, due to the law of large numbers, the distinction  
between $W$ and $\langle w\rangle$ is not important. Since thermodynamics  
is only interested in large systems, we only see $W$ in Eq (4.9).  
But from our calculations we find that, even for  
microscopic systems, the $2^{nd}$ law continues to hold. But  
it does so only our average.
