We have seen in $E_q$ (17) that for a quasistatic process,  
the work performed is exactly $\Delta F$. Thus, for a quasi-static  
process $\Omega(w) = P_R(-w)$, or, in words: if you go backwards the  
work performed is simply the original work. This is why we  
say that quasi-static processes are reversible.
