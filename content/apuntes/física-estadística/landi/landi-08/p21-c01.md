Now let's see what happens in the opposite limit, $T\to\infty$.

In this case we may approximate $e^{\beta\mu H}\approx 1$, for both  
$H>0$ and $H<0$.

The Gibbs probabilities become

$$\begin{aligned}
P_0 &= \frac{e^{\beta\mu H}}{2+e^{\beta\mu H}}\approx \frac{1}{3}
\end{aligned}\tag{18a}$$
