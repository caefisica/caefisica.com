$$\begin{aligned}
\langle e^{-\beta w}\rangle &= \int e^{-\beta w}P(w)\,dw = \int e^{-\beta w}P_R(w)e^{\beta(w-\Delta F)}\,dw\\
&= e^{-\beta\Delta F}\underbrace{\int P_R(w)\,dw}_{1}
\end{aligned}\tag{68}$$

Thus we concluded that

$$\boxed{\langle e^{-\beta w}\rangle=e^{-\beta\Delta F}}\tag{70}$$
