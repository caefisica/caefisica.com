Example : magnetic cooling

Samples may be cooled to around 3K by liquefying  
Helium. IF you want to go beyond that you will need  
more sophisticated tricks. magnetic cooling is one of  
those tricks, which can usually take you to around a  
few mK.

To use it you must give your sample to some paramagno-  
netic material. I will assume for simplicity that this  
material is spin 1/2, but that is not at all restrictive.  
we have seen that in this case

$$\begin{aligned} E_\sigma &= -\mu_B B \sigma \end{aligned}\tag{36}$$

and

$$\begin{aligned} Z &= 2\cosh\left(\frac{\mu_B B}{k_B T}\right) \end{aligned}\tag{37}$$
