This process may be modelled by a Markov Chain as

$$\begin{aligned}
P_n(t+1) &= \sum_m Q_{nm}(\lambda_t) P_m(t)
\end{aligned}\tag{52}$$

The difference is that now the transition probabilities will depend on time because they may depend on $\lambda$. We don't know what the $Q_{nm}$ are. All we impose is that they obey detailed balance for each value of $\lambda
