the matrix whose columns are the eigenvectors $\vec{A}_\ell$ is  
called the Fourier matrix (we forget about the $1/\sqrt{N}$)

$$\begin{aligned}
F_N &=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots\\
1 & \omega & \omega^2 & \omega^3 & \cdots\\
1 & \omega^2 & \omega^4 & \omega^6 & \cdots\\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots\\
1 & \omega^N & \omega^{2N} & \omega^{3N} & \cdots
\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}\tag{75}$$

or

$$\begin{aligned}
(F_N)_{\ell m} &= \omega^{\ell m}, \qquad \omega=e^{2\pi i/N}\\
\ell,m &= 0,\ldots,N-1
\end{aligned}\tag{78}$$
