The Planck distribution

Now that we know $g(\omega)$ we are ready to compute any  
$\mu$-sum we want. We start with the internal energy, $\hat{U}$ (6);

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= \sum_{k,\alpha} \frac{\hbar\omega_k}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega_k}-1}
= \int_0^\infty d\omega\,g(\omega)\frac{\hbar\omega}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
\end{aligned}\tag{16}$$
