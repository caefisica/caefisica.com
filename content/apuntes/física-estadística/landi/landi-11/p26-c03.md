Thus (48) becomes

$\frac{g}{m}(-e^{-ika}+2-e^{ika})B e^{ikx_m}=\omega^2B e^{ikx_m}$

cancelling out the term $B e^{ikx_m}$ we then get

$\omega^2=\frac{g}{m}(2-e^{ika}-e^{-ika})$

$=\frac{g}{m}(2-2\cos ka)$

Thus we conclude that the ansatz (52) indeed works, producing  
a frequency

$$\begin{aligned}
\omega_k&=\sqrt{\frac{2g}{m}(1-\cos ka)}
\end{aligned}\tag{53}$$

this is a dispersion relation; it relates frequency $\omega$ with  
wave-vector $k$.
