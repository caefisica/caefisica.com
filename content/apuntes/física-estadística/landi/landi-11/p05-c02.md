$$\sum_{k,\alpha} f(\omega_k)$$

where $f$ is an arbitrary function. It is convenient to convert this into an integral:

$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k,\alpha} f(\omega_k) &\longrightarrow \int_0^\infty d\omega\,g(\omega)f(\omega)
\end{aligned}\tag{12}$$

the quantity $g(\omega)$ is called the density of states (DOS). The procedure to find it is similar to the one we used when we discussed the ideal gas.

First we note that $\omega_k$ does not depend on $\alpha$ (the polarization)
