we may compute this integral by parts

$\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du$

choose $dv = x^2 dx$, $u = \ln(1-e^{-x})$, then

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\infty dx\,x^2\ln(1-e^{-x})
&= \underbrace{\left.\frac{x^3}{3}\ln(1-e^{-x})\right|_0^\infty}_{0}
-\int_0^\infty dx\,\frac{x^3}{3}\frac{(1+e^{-x})}{1-e^{-x}}\\
&= \frac{1}{3}\underbrace{\int_0^\infty dx\,\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}}_{\pi^4/15}
\end{aligned}\tag{20}$$

Thus

$F = -\frac{V}{\pi^2 c^3}\frac{k_B^4 T^4}{\hbar^3}\frac{1}{3}\frac{\pi^4}{15}$
