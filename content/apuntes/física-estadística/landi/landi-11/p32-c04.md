This shows that the speed of sound actually depends on the $k$ value. When $ka \ll 1$ then $v_g \simeq c$. But in the other extreme case, when $k \simeq \pm \frac{\pi}{a}$ then $v_g = 0$. Vibrations with really high momentum generate stationary waves (zero group velocity).
