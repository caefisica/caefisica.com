\underline{Free energy}, $\underline{E_q (q)}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
F &= k_B T \sum_{k,\alpha} \ln(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})
= k_B T \int_0^\infty d\omega\, g(\omega)\ln(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})\\
&= k_B T \frac{V}{\pi^2 c^3}\int_0^\infty d\omega\,\omega^2\ln(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})\\
&= k_B T \frac{V}{\pi^2 c^3}\frac{1}{(\beta\hbar)^3}\int_0^\infty dx\,x^2\ln(1-e^{-x})\\
&= \frac{V}{\pi^2 c^3}\frac{k_B T^4}{\hbar^3}\int_0^\infty dx\,x^2\ln(1-e^{-x})
\end{aligned}
$$

$x=\beta\hbar\omega$
