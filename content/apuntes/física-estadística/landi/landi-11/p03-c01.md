we are going to assume that the walls of our cavity are in  
thermal equilibrium at a given temperature $T$. Consequently,  
the $n(k,\alpha)$ will become random variables; there will be a  
continuous creation and annihilation of photons due to the  
thermal agitation of the atoms in the wall.  

what we want to ask, therefore, is what is the average photon  
number $\langle n(k,\alpha)\rangle$? well this is easy: this problem is mathematically  
analogous to the quantum harmonic oscillator.
