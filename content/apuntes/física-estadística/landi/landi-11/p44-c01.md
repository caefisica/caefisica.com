Now let

$$\begin{aligned}
\omega &= e^{2\pi i/N}
\end{aligned}\tag{72}$$

we thus get

$$\begin{aligned}
\vec{A}_\ell &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}
\begin{bmatrix}
\omega^\ell\\
\omega^{2\ell}\\
\omega^{3\ell}\\
\vdots\\
\omega^{N\ell}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}\tag{73}$$

If we choose to label the $N$ also as

$M \equiv 0,1,\ldots,N-1$

then

$$\begin{aligned}
A_{\ell m} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\omega^{\ell m}
\end{aligned}\tag{74}$$
