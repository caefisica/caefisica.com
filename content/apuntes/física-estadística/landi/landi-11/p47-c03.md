$$
\begin{bmatrix}
F_{N/2} & 0\\
0 & F_{N/2}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
I_{N/4} & D_{N/4} & 0 & 0\\
I_{N/4} & -D_{N/4} & 0 & 0\\
0 & 0 & I_{N/2} & D_{N/2}\\
0 & 0 & I_{N/2} & -D_{N/2}
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
F_{N/4}\\
F_{N/4}\\
F_{N/4}\\
F_{N/4}
\end{bmatrix}
\times
\begin{bmatrix}
0,4,8,\ldots\\
2,6,10,\ldots\\
1,5,9,\ldots\\
3,7,11,\ldots
\end{bmatrix}
$$

If $N=2^L$ this will require, in total $\frac{NL}{2}$ matrix multi-  
plications, instead of $N^2$.

Ex. $N=1024=2^{10}\Rightarrow L=10$.
