Internal energy

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= \frac{V}{\pi^2c^3}\int_0^\infty d\omega\,\frac{\hbar\omega^3}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
\qquad x=\beta\hbar\omega \qquad dx=\beta\hbar d\omega\\
&= \frac{V}{\pi^2c^3}\hbar\frac{1}{(\beta\hbar)^4}\int_0^\infty dx\,\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}
\end{aligned}$$

\int_0^\infty dx\,\frac{x^3}{e^x-1} &= \frac{\pi^4}{15}
