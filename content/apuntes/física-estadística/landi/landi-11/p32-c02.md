$$\begin{aligned}
\text{group velocity}: \quad v_k &= \frac{\partial \omega_k}{\partial k}
\end{aligned}\tag{61}$$

For our dispersion relation it is convenient to work

$1-\cos ka=2\sin^2\left(\frac{ka}{2}\right)$

Then we get

$$\begin{aligned}
\omega_k &= \sqrt{\frac{4g}{m}}\left|\sin\left(\frac{ka}{2}\right)\right|\\
\frac{\partial\omega_k}{\partial k} &= \sqrt{\frac{4g}{m}}\frac{a}{2}\cos\left(\frac{ka}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{62}$$
