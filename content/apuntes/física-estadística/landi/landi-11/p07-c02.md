$$\begin{aligned}
U &= \frac{V}{\pi^2c^3}\int_0^\infty d\omega\,\frac{\hbar\omega^3}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
\end{aligned}\tag{17}$$

we will do this integral in a minute. But first I want to  
analyze the integrand. we may write this formula as

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= V\int_0^\infty d\omega\,\rho(\omega)
\end{aligned}\tag{18}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\rho(\omega)=\frac{\hbar\omega^3}{\pi^2c^3}\frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}}
\end{aligned}\tag{19}$$

this quantity gives an energy per unit frequency. It is the fraction  
of the total energy which has frequency $\omega$. Eq (19) is known  
as the Planck distribution. It was proposed by Max Planck  
in 1900 and marks the birth of quantum mechanics.
