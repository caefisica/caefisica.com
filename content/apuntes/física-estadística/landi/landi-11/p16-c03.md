$k_B T\ln P_m=-E_m+k_B T-\alpha$

$\ln P_m=-\beta E_m+1-\beta\alpha$

$P_m=e^{-\beta E_m}\underbrace{(e^{1-\beta\alpha})}_{:=\frac{1}{Z}}$

The constant $\alpha$, or $1/Z$, must be set to ensure that $\sum_m P_m=1$.

It will then be the partition function

$$\begin{aligned}
P_m &= \frac{e^{-\beta E_m}}{Z}\\
Z &= \sum_m e^{-\beta E_m}
\end{aligned}\tag{34}$$
