$$\begin{aligned}
F &= \sum_m (E_m+k_BT\ln P_m)P_m+\alpha\left(1-\sum_m P_m\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{33}$$

We then fine $\alpha$ so as to ensure that $\sum_m P_m=1$.

Now we can compute $\partial F/\partial P_m$;

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial F}{\partial P_m}
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial P_m}\left\{\sum_\ell(E_\ell+k_BT\ln P_\ell)P_\ell+\alpha\left(1-\sum_\ell P_\ell\right)\right\}\\
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial P_m}\left[(E_m+k_BT\ln P_m)P_m\right]-\alpha\\
&= E_m+k_BT\ln P_m+k_BT\frac{P_m}{P_m}-\alpha\\
&= 0
\end{aligned}$$
