Recall

$E_1 = \hbar\omega m \qquad m = 0,1,2,3,\ldots$

$$\begin{aligned}
\Rightarrow Z_1 &= \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} e^{-\beta\hbar\omega m}
= \frac{1}{1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}}
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle m\rangle &= \frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
\end{aligned}\tag{4}$$

thus, the average occupation number $\langle m(k,\alpha)\rangle$ will be

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle m(k,\alpha)\rangle &= \frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega_k}-1}
\end{aligned}\tag{5}$$
