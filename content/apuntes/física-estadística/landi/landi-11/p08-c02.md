$d\omega=-\frac{2\pi c}{\lambda^2}d\lambda$

$\int_0^\infty d\omega\,\rho(\omega)=-2\pi c\int_\infty^0\frac{d\lambda}{\lambda^2}\rho(\omega(\lambda))=\int_0^\infty\frac{2\pi c\,d\lambda}{\lambda^2}\rho(\omega(\lambda))$

Thus

$\rho(\lambda)\,d\lambda=\rho(\omega)\,d\omega$

and

$\rho(\lambda)=\frac{2\pi c}{\lambda^2}\frac{\hbar}{\pi^2c^3}\left(\frac{2\pi c}{\lambda}\right)^3\frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar c2\pi/\lambda}-1}$
