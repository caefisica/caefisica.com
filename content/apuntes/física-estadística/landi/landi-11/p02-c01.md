Photons in a box

A photon is completely characterized by its wave vector $k$ and its polarization $\alpha = 1,2$ (you can take 1 for x-polarized and 2 for y-polarized. Or you can use circular polarization; it makes no difference), the dispersion relation for photons is

$$\begin{aligned}
\omega_k &= c \lvert k \rvert
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
