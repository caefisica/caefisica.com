The 1D harmonic chain

Now consider the following problem :

$a$

$0 \quad x_1 \quad x_2 \quad x_3 \quad x_4 \quad \cdots \quad x_N \to x$

we have a "1D crystal" of $N$ atoms, we denote by $x_m$ the equilibrium position of each atom. So if we define the lattice spacing $a$, then

$$
x_m = a m \qquad
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
x_1 &= a\\
x_2 &= 2a\\
\vdots
\end{aligned}
\right.
$$

the $x_m$ are fixed. they are the positions the atoms would have if they were standing still. we now let $X_m$ be the actual position.

we assume that each atom interacts only with its two nearest neighbors. So atom $m$ will interact with $m-1$ and $m+1$. the interaction has the form

$$V(X_m-X_{m+1}) \simeq V(a) + \frac{g}{2}(X_m-X_{m+1}-a)^2$$

It is more convenient to define the relative displacement

$$q_m = X_m - x_m$$
