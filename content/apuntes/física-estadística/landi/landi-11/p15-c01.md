Random topic: equilibrium is the state which minimizes  
the free energy $F = U - TS$.

Sorry, I completely forgot to discuss an important interpretation  
of fractional states. Suppose you have a system with some probabilities  
$P_m$. You don't know what they are. they may or may not be equilibri-  
brium probabilities.

We can always define the average energy as

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= \sum_m E_m P_m
\end{aligned}\tag{29}$$

and the entropy as

$$\begin{aligned}
S &= -k_B \sum_m \ln P_m
\end{aligned}\tag{30}$$
