The integral over $d\Omega$ gives $4\pi$ so

$2\sum_k f(\omega_k)=2\frac{V}{(2\pi)^3}4\pi\int_0^\infty dk\,k^2f(\omega_k)$

Finally, we change the integration variable from $k$ to $\omega=ck$. We

will then have

$k^2dk=\left(\frac{\omega}{c}\right)^2\frac{d\omega}{c}=\frac{\omega^2d\omega}{c^3}$
