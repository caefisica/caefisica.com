Now let's try to construct the classical equations of  
motion for the system, they read

$$\begin{aligned}
m\ddot{q}_m &= F_m = -\frac{\partial V}{\partial q_m}
\end{aligned}\tag{4}$$

Let's compute this derivative carefully:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V ={}& \cdots + \frac{g}{2}(q_{m-2}-q_{m-1})^2 + \frac{g}{2}(q_{m-1}-q_m)^2 + \frac{g}{2}(q_m-q_{m+1})^2\\
&+ \frac{g}{2}(q_{m+1}-q_{m+2})^2 + \cdots
\end{aligned}
$$

So, when we apply $\partial/\partial q_m$, it will only act on two of these terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial V}{\partial q_m}
&= \frac{g}{2}\left\{\frac{\partial}{\partial q_m}(q_{m-1}-q_m)^2+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_m}(q_m-q_{m+1})^2\right\}\\
&= \frac{g}{2}\left\{2(q_{m-1}-q_m)(-1)+2(q_m-q_{m+1})(1)\right\}\\
&= g(q_{m-1}-2q_m+q_{m+1})
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the equations of motion will be

$$\begin{aligned}
m\ddot{q}_m &= g(q_{m-1}-2q_m+q_{m+1})
\end{aligned}\tag{72}$$

these are couple equations. If $N=10^{23}$ then we would have to  
simultaneously solve all $10^{23}$ equations. So at first, this seems  
like a hard problem. It is a remarkable fact that these  
equations can actually be solved exactly. This is a consequence  
of the harmonic approximation. If we had not used it, we  
would be in real trouble.
