Before we try to solve Eq (12) let us derive it in a fancier  
way. We may define a Lagrangian for our system

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L} &= \sum_{m=1}^{N} \frac{M}{2}\dot{q}_m^2 - \frac{K}{2}\sum_{m=1}^{N}(q_m-q_{m+1})^2
\end{aligned}\tag{43}$$

we now apply the Euler - Lagrange equations

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot{q}_m}\right)-\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial q_m} &= 0
\end{aligned}\tag{44}$$
