We can also go beyond; the total number of photons will  
be

$$\begin{aligned}
N &= \sum_{k,\alpha}\langle n(k,\alpha)\rangle = \sum_{k,\alpha}\frac{1}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega_k}-1}
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

The partition function will be a product of the individual  
partition functions

$$\begin{aligned}
Z &= \prod_{k,\alpha}\left[\frac{1}{1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega_k}}\right]
\end{aligned}\tag{8}$$

Thus, the free energy will be a sum

$$\begin{aligned}
F &= -k_BT\ln Z = k_BT\sum_{k,\alpha}\ln(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega_k})
\end{aligned}\tag{9}$$

If we have $F$, then we can compute the entropy as

$$\begin{aligned}
S &= \frac{U-F}{T}
\end{aligned}\tag{10}$$
