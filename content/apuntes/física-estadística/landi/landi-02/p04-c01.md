The uniform distribution

The uniform distribution $\operatorname{Unif}(a,b)$ is defined as

$$\begin{aligned}
X \sim \operatorname{Unif}(a,b):\quad p(x) &=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{b-a}, & a \leq x \leq b\\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\tag{10}$$

Of particular importance is $\operatorname{Unif}(0,1)$, also called the standard uniform.

The CDF is found from the definition (7)

$F(x) = P(X < x) = \int_{-\infty}^{x} p(y)\,dy = \int_{a}^{x} \dfrac{1}{b-a}\,dy$
