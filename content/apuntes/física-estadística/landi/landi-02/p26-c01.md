The lognormal distribution

when you produce micro or nanoparticles, for instance in  
chemical or pharmaceutical processes, each particle will usually  
have a different diameter. Thus, the diameter of a nanopar-  
ticle is a random variable. usually the diameter follows  
a lognormal distribution.

the word: “lognormal” means that “the log is normal”. That  
is, if $Y$ is lognormal than $X = \ln Y$ will be normal. We may  
find the PDF of $Y$ starting with $X \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and $Y = e^X$.
