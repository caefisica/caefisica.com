2) $(x,y)$ are uniform on a circle;

$x^2+y^2=1$

the area of the circle in $\pi$ so the joint dist. is

$$\begin{aligned}
p_{X,Y}(x,y)&=
\begin{cases}
\frac{1}{\pi} & x^2+y^2=1\\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\tag{5.5}$$

But now $x$ and $y$ are not independent. For instance

$$\begin{aligned}
x=0 &\Rightarrow -1\leq Y\leq 1\\
x=1 &\Rightarrow Y=0
\end{aligned}$$
