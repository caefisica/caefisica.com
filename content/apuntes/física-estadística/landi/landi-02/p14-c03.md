Thus :

$$\begin{aligned}
x &\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2) : &\langle x\rangle &= \mu\\
&&\operatorname{Var}(x) &= \sigma^2
\end{aligned}\tag{33}$$

To find the distribution it is easier to start with

$$F(x)=P(X\leq x)=P(\mu+\sigma z\leq x)=P\left(z\leq\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)$$

Looking at (26) we will then have

$$F(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}}e^{-y^2/2}\,dy$$
