Let us marginalize on $X$;

$$\begin{aligned}
p_X(x) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\sqrt{1-x^2}}^{+\sqrt{1-x^2}}dy = \frac{2}{\pi}\sqrt{1-x^2}
\end{aligned}$$

Thus

$$\begin{aligned}
p_X(x) &= \frac{2}{\pi}\sqrt{1-x^2} \qquad x\in[-1,1]
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
