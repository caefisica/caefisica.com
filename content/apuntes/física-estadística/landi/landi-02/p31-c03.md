$\hat{p}_{Y|X}(y|x) = \frac{\hat{p}_{X,Y}(x,y)}{\hat{p}_X(x)} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{1-x^2}}$

This will hold for $-\sqrt{1-x^2} \leq y \leq \sqrt{1-x^2}$. Thus, if we  
know that $x=x_1$, $Y$ will be uniform

$$\begin{aligned}
Y \mid X=x_1 &\sim \mathrm{Unif}\left(-\sqrt{1-x^2},\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{17}$$
