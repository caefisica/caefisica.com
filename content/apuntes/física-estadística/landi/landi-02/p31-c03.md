$p_{Y|X}(y|x)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_X(x)}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{1-x^2}}$

This will hold for $-\sqrt{1-x^2}\leq y\leq\sqrt{1-x^2}$. Thus, if we

Y\mid X=x\sim\operatorname{Unif}\left(-\sqrt{1-x^2},\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)
