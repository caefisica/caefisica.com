Example : generating Expo $(\lambda)$.

The CDF of Expo $(\lambda)$ in [Eq (17)]

$F(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x}$

we now find the inverse : $F(x)=u \Longrightarrow x=F^{-1}(u)$

$e^{-\lambda x}=1-u$

$-\lambda x=\ln(1-u)$

$x=-\frac{1}{\lambda}\ln(1-u)$
