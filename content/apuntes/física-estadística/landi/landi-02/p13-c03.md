Thus, the variance is

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(z)=1}
\end{aligned}\tag{30}$$

The numbers appearing in $N(0,1)$ are therefore the mean and the variance.

The CDF of $N(0,1)$ is

$$\begin{aligned}
F(z)&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{z}e^{-y^2/2}\,dy
=\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{erfc}\left(-\frac{z}{\sqrt{2}}\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{31}$$

This integral cannot be expressed in terms of elementary functions. It is written in terms of the complex error function $\operatorname{erfc}(x)$.
