The normal $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$

The more general normal is constructed from the $N(0,1)$

as

$$\begin{aligned}
x &= \mu + \sigma z \qquad z \sim N(0,1)
\end{aligned}\tag{3.2}$$

where $\mu,\sigma$ are two parameters. The mean is

$\langle x\rangle=\mu+\sigma\langle z\rangle=\mu$
