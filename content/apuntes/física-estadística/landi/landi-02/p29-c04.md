Finally, we define conditional probability as

$$\begin{aligned}
p_{X,Y}(x,y) &= p_{X|Y}(x|y)\,p_Y(y)\\
&= p_{Y|X}(y|x)\,p_X(x)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.6}$$

The interpretation is the same as in the discrete case
