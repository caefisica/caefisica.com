The exponential distribution

The exponential distribution $\mathrm{Expo}(\lambda)$ is defined by

$$\begin{aligned}
X &\sim \mathrm{Expo}(\lambda), \quad p(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}, \quad x>0
\end{aligned}\tag{17}$$
