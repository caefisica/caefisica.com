If we eventually get tired of one of them, we may compute the marginal distribution by integrating over one of them:

$$\begin{aligned}
p_X(x) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy\\
p_Y(y) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx
\end{aligned}\tag{5.3}$$

Notice that this is a one way process: from the joint you get the marginal, but from the marginal there is no way of getting back the joint.
