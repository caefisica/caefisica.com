PDF and CDF

When x is a random variable that varies continually,  
instead of probabilities we work with the probability  
density function (PDF)

$$\begin{aligned}
p(x) \quad \text{or} \quad p_X(x) &= \mathrm{PDF}
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
