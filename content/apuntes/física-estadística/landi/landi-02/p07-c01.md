$$\begin{aligned}
\langle x\rangle &= -\lambda\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda}\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda x}\,dx
= -\lambda\frac{\partial}{\partial\lambda}\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}\right)
= -\lambda\left(-\frac{1}{\lambda^2}\right)
= \frac{1}{\lambda}\\
\langle x\rangle &= \frac{1}{\lambda}
\end{aligned}\tag{1.9}$$

we use the same trick to compute the second moment

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle x^2\rangle &= \lambda\int_0^\infty x^2e^{-\lambda x}\,dx
= \lambda\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\lambda^2}\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda x}\,dx\\
&= \lambda\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\lambda^2}\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}\right)
\end{aligned}$$
