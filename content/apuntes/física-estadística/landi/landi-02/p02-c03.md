$$\begin{aligned}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p(x)\,dx &= 1
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$

which is the normalization condition for PDFs.

If you look for the prob. of finding $x$ in a small interval $\left[x-\frac{\Delta x}{2},x+\frac{\Delta x}{2}\right]$ we get

$$\begin{aligned}
P\left(x-\frac{\Delta x}{2}\leq x\leq x+\frac{\Delta x}{2}\right)
&= \int_{x-\frac{\Delta x}{2}}^{x+\frac{\Delta x}{2}} p(x)\,dx \simeq p(x)\Delta x
\end{aligned}\tag{4}$$

Note that $p(x)$ is a density, so if $x$ has units of length, then $p(x)$ will have units of 1/length. this is most evident from (2) or (3) since probabilities have no units. Note also that $p(x)>0$, but it does not have to be smaller than 1.
