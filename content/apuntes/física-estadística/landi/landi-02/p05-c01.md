Next we compute the average:

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle x\rangle &= \int_a^b \frac{1}{b-a}x\,dx
= \frac{1}{b-a}\left(\frac{b^2}{2}-\frac{a^2}{2}\right)
= \frac{1}{2}\frac{(b-a)(b+a)}{b-a} \\
\text{Thus:}\qquad \langle x\rangle &= \frac{b+a}{2}
\end{aligned}\tag{14}$$

And the 2nd moment

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle x^2\rangle &= \frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b x^2\,dx
= \frac{1}{b-a}\left(\frac{b^3-a^3}{3}\right)
= \frac{1}{3}\frac{(b-a)(a^2+ab+b^2)}{b-a} \\
\text{Thus}\qquad \langle x^2\rangle &= \frac{a^2+ab+b^2}{3}
\end{aligned}\tag{15}$$
