Random walk

Summing random variables in the basic idea of a  
random walk. If $x_i$ is the step the walker takes at time $i$,  
then after a time $t$ the walker will be at position

$$\begin{aligned}
y_t &= x_1 + x_2 + \ldots + x_t
\end{aligned}\tag{49}$$
