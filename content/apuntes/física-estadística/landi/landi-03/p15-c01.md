Now let $Y = X_1 + \cdots + X_m$, where the $X_i$ are independent r. v. s

the CF of $Y$ is given in (17) so the C G F. will be

$$\begin{aligned}
C_Y(s) &= \ln G_Y(s) = \ln G_{X_1} + \cdots + \ln G_{X_m} \\
\text{or}\qquad C_Y(s) &= C_{X_1}(s) + \cdots + C_{X_m}(s)
\end{aligned}\tag{30}$$
