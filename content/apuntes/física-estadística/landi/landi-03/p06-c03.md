$G(s)=1+is\langle z\rangle+\frac{(is)^2}{2!}\langle z^2\rangle+\frac{(is)^3}{3!}\langle z^3\rangle+\frac{(is)^4}{4!}\langle z^4\rangle+\cdots$

First, we readily see that there are no terms which are odd in

$s$ and, consequently,

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle z\rangle=\langle z^3\rangle=\langle z^5\rangle=\cdots
\end{aligned}\tag{12}$$

As for the even moments, we see that

$\langle z^2\rangle=1$

$\langle z^4\rangle=\frac{4!}{2!2^2}=3$
