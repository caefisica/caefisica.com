Thus the CF of $Y_t$ will be

$$\begin{aligned}
G_{Y_t}(s) &= \left(p_0 + e^{is}p_+ + e^{-is}p_-\right)^t
\end{aligned}\tag{55}$$

we could try to use the inverse Fourier transform to find the PDF of $Y_t$ [Eq (2)]. But that will turn out to be quite complicated. To see why, let us look at $t=2$ (only two steps).

$$\begin{aligned}
G_{Y_2}(s) &= \left(p_0 + e^{is}p_+ + e^{-is}p_-\right)^2 \\
&= p_0^2 + p_+p_- + e^{2is}p_+^2 + e^{-2is}p_-^2 + 2p_0p_+e^{is} + 2p_0p_-e^{-is}
\end{aligned}\tag{56}$$
