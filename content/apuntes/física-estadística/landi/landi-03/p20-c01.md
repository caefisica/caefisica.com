\underline{Example} : $Bin (m,p)$ and $Pois (\lambda)$

We know that $Bin (m,p)$ is the sum of $m$ Bernoulli  
trials, that is, if $X_i \sim Bern (p)$ then

$$\begin{aligned}
Y &= X_1 + \cdots + X_m \sim Bin (m,p)
\end{aligned}\tag{17}$$
