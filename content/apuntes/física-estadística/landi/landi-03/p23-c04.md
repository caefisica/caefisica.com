You can probably see that for $t = 3, 4, 5, \ldots$ things will start to get quite complicated.

We may consider instead the case of large $t$. According to the central limit theorem, in this case we may approximate $Y_t$ by a Gaussian. the parameters of the distribution we already know; they are given in (53). Thus, the PDF of $Y_t$ will be

$$\begin{aligned}
P_{Y_t}(y) &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2 t}}\exp\left\{-\frac{(y-\mu t)^2}{2\sigma^2 t}\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{59}$$
