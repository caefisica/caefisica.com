The central limit theorem states that, when $N$ is large

$Y$ will tend to a normal distribution

$$\begin{aligned}
N\text{ very large:}\quad Y &\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)
\end{aligned}\tag{36}$$

In words: if you sum a bunch of r.v.s the distribution  
becomes approximately normal. The parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$ of  
the distribution are very easy to figure out since they are
