Now we look for the PDF using Eq. (2):

$$
\phi(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} ds \, e^{-isx} G(s)
$$

$$
= \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} ds \, e^{-isx} (q + p e^{is})^m
$$

To compute this integral we expand the term $(q+p e^{is})^m$ using the Binomial theorem:

$$
\begin{aligned}
e^{-isx}(q + p e^{is})^m
&= e^{-isx}\sum_{k=0}^{m} \binom{m}{k}(p e^{is})^k q^{m-k} \\
&= \sum_{k=0}^{m} \binom{m}{k} p^k q^{m-k} e^{is(k-x)}
\end{aligned}
$$
