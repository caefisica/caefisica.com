As we discussed around Eq (17), it is also easy to find the CF. But the PDF in general is quite complicated. However, if the number of steps is large we may invoke the central limit theorem and approximate the PDF of $Y_t$ to a Gaussian.

Let me illustrate these ideas with a concrete example.

Suppose that the walker may take a step to the right with prob. $p_+$, to the left with prob. $p_-$ or stay still with prob. $1-p_+-p_-$. That is

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x_i=1) &= p_+\\
P(x_i=-1) &= p_-\\
P(x_i=0) &= 1-p_+-p_- = p_0
\end{aligned}\tag{51}$$
