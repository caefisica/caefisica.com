We then have

$G_X(s) = \langle e^{isX} \rangle = \langle e^{is(\mu+\sigma Z)} \rangle = e^{is\mu}\langle e^{is\sigma Z} \rangle$

The last term is the CF of z, but with parameter $\sigma s$ instead of $s$. That is,

$\langle e^{is\sigma Z} \rangle = G_Z(s\sigma) = e^{-(s\sigma)^2/2}$
