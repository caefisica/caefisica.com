This means that we must either have a symmetric wave-function,

$$\begin{aligned}
\psi(r_1,r_2)&=\psi(r_2,r_1)
\end{aligned}\tag{5}$$

which is what we call a symmetric wave-function, or

$$\begin{aligned}
\psi(r_1,r_2)&=-\psi(r_2,r_1)
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$

which is what we call an anti-symmetric wave-function.
