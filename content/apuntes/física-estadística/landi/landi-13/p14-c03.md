If we allow for the number of particles to fluctuate, then we include in Eq (33) a term of the form

$$\begin{aligned}
dU &= T\,dS - p\,dV + \mu\,dN
\end{aligned}\tag{35}$$

where $\mu$ is called the chemical potential. It's physical meaning is made explicit if we write

$$\begin{aligned}
\mu &= \left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial N}\right)_{S,V}
\end{aligned}\tag{36}$$

It represents the 'change in energy when we introduce an additional particle in our system, at fixed volume and entropy (no heat is exchanged).
