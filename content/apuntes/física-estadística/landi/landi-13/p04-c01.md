Protons, neutrons and electrons all have spin $1/2$. Helium -4  
has 2 of each, giving a total spin of $3$. Hence it behaves as a  
Boson. But Helium -3 has one neutron less so it's spin is $5/2$,  
and it behaves like a. Fermion, we will learn that Bosons  
and Fermions behave quite differently: $He^4$ can become superfluid  
but $He^3$ cannot.
