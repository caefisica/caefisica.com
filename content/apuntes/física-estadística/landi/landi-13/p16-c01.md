For the grand canonical ensemble, we will not work with  
free energy. Instead we work with the thermodynamic  
potential

$$\begin{aligned}
\Phi &= F-\mu N = U-TS-\mu N
\end{aligned}\tag{40}$$

we get

$d\Phi=dF-\mu\,dN-N\,d\mu$

$=(-S\,dT-p\,dV+\mu\,dN)-\mu\,dN-N\,d\mu$
