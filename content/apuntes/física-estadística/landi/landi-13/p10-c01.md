Contact with a heat bath

Suppose now that our system is placed in contact with  
a heat bath, this means that the $n_j$ will become random  
variables which will stay in constant fluctuations. For  
instance, at some given instant the bath may give a kick  
in a particle and increase its momentum from $k$ to $k+\Delta k$.  
this means that $n_k$ is reduced by one and $n_{k+\Delta k}$ is increased  
by 1. Hence, due to processes such as this, the numbers $n_j$ will  
keep on fluctuating.

the quantity we are interested in is the joint probability  
distribution

$$\begin{aligned}
P(n_1,n_2,\ldots) &:= P(\{n_j\})
\end{aligned}\tag{24}$$

which gives the proba. of finding each $n_j$ at a different number.  
If the system is in thermal equilibrium then we already  
know the answer.

$$\begin{aligned}
P(\{n_j\}) &= \frac{e^{-\beta E}}{Z}
\end{aligned}\tag{25}$$
