Note that this is not $(z_1)^2$ because $z_1^2$ would have

$e^{-\beta(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)}$ and $e^{-\beta(\epsilon_2+\epsilon_1)}$.

If we keep going things just get more and more complicated. this is why we gave up on the canonical ensemble and went instead to the grand canonical case.
