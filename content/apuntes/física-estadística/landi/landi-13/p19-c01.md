Thus we have a Lagrange multiplier method, which imposes normalization.

$$\begin{aligned}
\Phi' &= \Phi + \alpha\left(1-\sum_{j m_j l} P(3m_j l)\right)
\end{aligned}\tag{49}$$

where $\alpha$ is a Lagrange multiplier. Then, the condition

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial \Phi'}{\partial \alpha} &= 0 \quad \leadsto \quad 1-\sum_{j m_j l} P(3m_j l)=0
\end{aligned}\tag{50}$$

which fixes the normalization.
