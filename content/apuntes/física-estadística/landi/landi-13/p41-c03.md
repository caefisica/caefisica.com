Recall that in the classical limit $z=e^{\beta\mu}\ll1$, thus the  
first order solution will be

$$\begin{aligned}
e^{\beta\mu}=z&=\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}
\end{aligned}\tag{93}$$

or

$$\begin{aligned}
\beta\mu&=\ln\left\{\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{94}$$
