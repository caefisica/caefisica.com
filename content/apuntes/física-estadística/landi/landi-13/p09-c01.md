For a bosonic system each $N_j$ may have the values

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Bosons}: \quad N_j &= 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots
\end{aligned}\tag{20}$$

But for Fermions the Pauli principle imposes that

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Fermions}: \quad n_j &= 0, 1
\end{aligned}\tag{21}$$
