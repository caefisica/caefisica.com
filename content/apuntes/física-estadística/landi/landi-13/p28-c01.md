The classical limit

we must be able, somehow, to recover classical results  
from our formalism: By "classical", what we mean in a  
situation where the distinction between Fermions and  
Bosons becomes unimportant.

We may exploit the results for Bosons and Fermions  
together as:

$$\Phi = \pm k_B T \sum_j \ln\left(1 \mp e^{-\beta(\epsilon_j-\mu)}\right)\tag{61}$$

$$\overline{N}_j = \frac{1}{e^{\beta(\epsilon_j-\mu)} \mp 1}\tag{62}$$
