Equilibrium in the minimum of the thermodynamic potential

we know that the canonical ensemble was the state which minimizes the free energy $F = U - TS$. But now the number of particles may also fluctuate, so equilibrium must be determined from a competition of $U$, $-TS$ and the energy cost involved in changing the number of particles, which is $-\mu N$. thus, in the grand canonical ensemble, equilibrium will be the state which minimizes the thermodynamic potential

$$\begin{aligned}
\Phi &= U - TS - \mu N
\end{aligned}\tag{46}$$

Our system is described by the probabilities $P(\{m_j\})$ so

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= \sum_{\{m_j\}} \left(\sum_j \epsilon_j m_j\right) P(\{m_j\})
\end{aligned}\tag{47a}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
S &= - k_B \sum_{\{m_j\}} P(\{m_j\}) \ln P(\{m_j\})
\end{aligned}\tag{47b}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
N &= \sum_{\{m_j\}} \left(\sum_j m_j\right) P(\{m_j\})
\end{aligned}\tag{47c}$$
