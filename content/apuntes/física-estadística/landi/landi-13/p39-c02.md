$\bar n(\epsilon)=\frac{1}{e^{\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}\mp1}=e^{-\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}\frac{1}{1\mp e^{-\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}}$

then, since $e^{-\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}\ll1$ we may expand

$\frac{1}{1\pm x}\simeq1\mp x$

To get

$\bar n(\epsilon)\simeq e^{-\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}\left[1\mp e^{-\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}\right]$
