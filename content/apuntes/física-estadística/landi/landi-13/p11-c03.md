$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{m_1,m_2,\ldots}\rho(m_j)&=1
\end{aligned}\tag{28}$$

where the sum is over all possible many-body configurations.

Inserting (27) we may also write this as

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{Z}\sum_{m_1,m_2,\ldots}e^{-\beta E}\delta\left(\sum_jm_j=N\right)&=1
\end{aligned}\tag{29}$$

where $\delta(a=b)$ is the Kronecter $\delta$ (written in a slightly different way)

$$\begin{aligned}
\delta(a=b)&=
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{if } a=b\\
0 & \text{if } a\ne b
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\tag{30}$$
