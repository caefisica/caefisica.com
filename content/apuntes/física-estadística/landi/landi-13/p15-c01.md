$U$ is a function of $(S,V,N)$. If it were convenient to work  
with temperature instead of entropy, so we make a Legendre  
transformation to define the Helmholtz free energy

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{F = U - TS}
\end{aligned}\tag{37}$$

we now have

$$\begin{aligned}
dF &= dU - T dS - S dT\\
&= (T dS - p dV + \mu dN) - T dS - S dT
\end{aligned}$$
