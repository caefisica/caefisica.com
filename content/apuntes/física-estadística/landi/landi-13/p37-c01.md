The total number of particles in a particular microstate is

$$\begin{aligned}
N&:=\sum_{k,\lambda}\bar{m}_{k,\lambda}
\end{aligned}\tag{82}$$

and the total energy is

$$\begin{aligned}
U&:=\sum_{k,\lambda}\epsilon_k\bar{m}_{k,\lambda}
\end{aligned}\tag{83}$$
