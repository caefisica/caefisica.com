Let $\Psi(r_1,r_2)$ be the wave function for two identical particles. The observable quantity is $|\Psi|^2$. Thus, since they are indistinguishable, we must have

$$\begin{aligned}
|\Psi(r_1,r_2)|^2 &= |\Psi(r_2,r_1)|^2
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$

This implies that the two wave-functions may differ by at most a phase factor

$$\begin{aligned}
\Psi(r_2,r_1) &= e^{i\theta}\Psi(r_1,r_2)
\end{aligned}\tag{2}$$
