$z=\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}\pm\frac{z^2}{2^{3/2}}$

$\tilde z\simeq\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}\pm\frac{1}{2^{3/2}}\left[\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}\right]^2$

Thus

$$\begin{aligned}
\beta\mu&\simeq\ln\left\{\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}
\left[1\pm\frac{1}{2^{3/2}}\frac{\lambda^3N/V}{(2S+1)}\right]\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{79}$$

Note that for Fermions the chemical potential is larger than the classical case, whereas for Bosons it is smaller.
