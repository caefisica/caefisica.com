Next we look at the energy, $E_0$ (87). We have.

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= \alpha V \int_0^\infty d\epsilon\ \epsilon^{3/2}\ \bar{m}(\epsilon)\\
&= \alpha V \int_0^\infty d\epsilon\ \epsilon^{3/2}\left\{e^{-\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}+e^{-2\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}\right\}\\
&= \alpha V\left\{e^{\beta\mu}\frac{3\sqrt{\pi}}{4\beta^{5/2}}+\frac{e^{2\beta\mu}3\sqrt{\pi}}{4(2\beta)^{5/2}}\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{97}$$
