For Bosons Eq (56) gives

$$\begin{aligned}
\bar{n}_j &= -\frac{\partial}{\partial(\beta E_j)}\ln\left[\frac{1}{1-e^{-\beta(E_j-\mu)}}\right]\\
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial(\beta E_j)}\ln\left(1-e^{-\beta(E_j-\mu)}\right)\\
&= \frac{e^{-\beta(E_j-\mu)}}{1-e^{-\beta(E_j-\mu)}}
\end{aligned}$$
