However, this $\Psi$ is neither symmetric nor antisymmetric.

If particle 1 is a proton and particle 2 is an electron, then (12) is a perfectly fine wave-function. But if both are protons or both are electrons, then the wave-function $\underline{\text{must}}$ be symmetrical or anti - symmetrical.

A possible symmetric wave-function may be

$$\begin{aligned}
\Psi_S(\vec r_1,\vec r_2)&:=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left\{\phi_{n_1}(\vec r_1)\phi_{n_2}(\vec r_2)+\phi_{n_1}(\vec r_2)\phi_{n_2}(\vec r_1)\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{13}$$
