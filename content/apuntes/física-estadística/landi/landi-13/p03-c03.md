$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Spin} &= 0,\frac{1}{2},1,\frac{3}{2},2,\frac{5}{2},\cdots
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

the division found by Pauli is

Integer spin: $0,1,2,\ldots$ $\Rightarrow$ $\psi$ symmetric $\Rightarrow$ Bosons

e.g.: photons, Hibles boson, graviton, $^4\mathrm{He}$

mod.

Half-integer spin: $\frac{1}{2},\frac{3}{2},\frac{5}{2},\ldots$ $\Rightarrow$ $\psi$ anti-symmetric $\Rightarrow$ Fermions

e.g.: protons, electrons, neutrons, $^3\mathrm{He}$
