Or, more generally,

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Cov}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m} a_iX_i,\sum_{j=1}^{m} b_jY_j\right)
&=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}a_ib_j\mathrm{Cov}(X_i,Y_j)
\end{aligned}\tag{4}$$

This property may be combined with (2a) to give the following important result:

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Var}(X_1+X_2)
&=\mathrm{Cov}(X_1+X_2,X_1+X_2)\\
&=\mathrm{Cov}(X_1,X_1)+\mathrm{Cov}(X_1,X_2)+\mathrm{Cov}(X_2,X_1)+\mathrm{Cov}(X_2,X_2)\\
&=\mathrm{Var}(X_1)+2\mathrm{Cov}(X_1,X_2)+\mathrm{Var}(X_2)
\end{aligned}$$
