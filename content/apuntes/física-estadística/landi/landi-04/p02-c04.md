$$\begin{aligned}
\text{(d)}\quad \operatorname{Cov}(cx,y)&=c\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)
\end{aligned}\tag{2d}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{(e)}\quad \operatorname{Cov}(x,y+z)&=\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)+\operatorname{Cov}(x,z)
\end{aligned}\tag{2e}$$

Properties (d) and (e) say that cov is bilinear: with one r. v.  
held constant, cov is linear in the other.

We may generalize (e) as follows

$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}(x+y,z+w)&=\operatorname{Cov}(x+y,z)+\operatorname{Cov}(x+y,w)\\
&=\operatorname{Cov}(x,z)+\operatorname{Cov}(y,z)+\operatorname{Cov}(x,w)+\operatorname{Cov}(y,w)
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$
