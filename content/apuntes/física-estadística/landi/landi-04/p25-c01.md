Example : PDF of the multivariate normal

Let $z_1,\ldots,z_N \sim N(0,1)$ iid. we saw that a general  
multivariate Gaussian may be defined as

$$
x = Qz + \mu
\tag{67}
$$

Now let us find the PDF of $X$, starting from the PDF of $z$.  
Since the $z_i$ are independent

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_z(z) &= P_{z_1,\ldots,z_N}(z_1,\ldots,z_N)
= P_{z_1}(z_1)\cdots P_{z_N}(z_N)\\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^N}}\exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2}
(z_1^2+\cdots+z_N^2)\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{68}
$$

we may write this more compactly as

$$
P_z(z)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^N}}\exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2}z^Tz\right\}
\tag{69}
$$
