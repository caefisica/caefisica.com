Correlation

If $x,y$ have units, $\operatorname{cov}(x,y)$ will also have units. It's convenient to introduce a dimensionless measure of correlation.

A convenient definition is

$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Corr}(x,y) &= \frac{\operatorname{cov}(x,y)}{\operatorname{SD}(x)\operatorname{SD}(y)}
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

where $\operatorname{SD}(x)=\sqrt{\operatorname{var}(x)}$ is the standard deviation. The correlation will be a number between $-1$ and $1$ (proof below):

$$\begin{aligned}
-1 \leq \operatorname{Corr}(x,y) \leq 1
\end{aligned}\tag{8}$$
