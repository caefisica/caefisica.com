Note that the diagonals are the variances. So if $X_1,\ldots,X_N$ are independent, the covariance matrix would be diagonal. Note also that, since $\operatorname{Cov}(X_2,X_1)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)$, $\Theta$ will be a symmetric matrix.

$$\begin{aligned}
\Theta^{\mathsf{T}} &= \Theta
