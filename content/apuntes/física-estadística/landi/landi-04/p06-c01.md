The covariance matrix

Say you have a bunch of r.v. s $X_1,\ldots,X_N$, and you want to study their covariances. It is useful to group them in a matrix, with entries

$$\begin{aligned}
\Theta_{ij} &= \operatorname{Cov.}(X_i,X_j)
\end{aligned}\tag{0}$$
