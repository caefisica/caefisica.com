$$\begin{aligned}
x &= \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ \vdots \\ x_N \end{bmatrix}
&\qquad x^T &= [x_1\ \cdots\ x_N]
\end{aligned}\tag{13}$$

The inner (or dot) product may then be written as

$$\begin{aligned}
x^T x &= [x_1\ \cdots\ x_N]\begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ \vdots \\ x_N \end{bmatrix}\\
&= x_1^2+\cdots+x_N^2
\end{aligned}\tag{14}$$
