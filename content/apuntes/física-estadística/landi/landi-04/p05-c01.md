The inequality (8) may also be rewritten as

$$\begin{aligned}
[\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)]^2 &\le \operatorname{Var}(x)\operatorname{Var}(y)
\end{aligned}\tag{9}$$

This is a manifestation of the Cauchy - Schwarz inequality. To prove it we define a new r.v.

$$z=x-cy$$

where $c$ is a constant using (5) we then get

$$\operatorname{Var}(z)=\operatorname{Var}(x)+c^2\operatorname{Var}(y)-2c\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)\ge 0$$

since $\operatorname{Var}(z)\ge 0$. Now choose $c=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)}{\operatorname{Var}(y)}$, then we get

$$\operatorname{Var}(x)+\left[\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)}{\operatorname{Var}(y)}\right]^2\operatorname{Var}(y)-2\left[\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)}{\operatorname{Var}(y)}\right]\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)\ge 0$$

or

$$\operatorname{Var}(x)-\frac{[\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)]^2}{\operatorname{Var}(y)}\ge 0$$

which is precisely Eq (9). qed.
