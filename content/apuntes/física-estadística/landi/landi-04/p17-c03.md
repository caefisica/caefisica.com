$$\begin{aligned}
\langle T\rangle &= \lim_{\sigma\to\infty}\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\sigma}^{\sigma}\frac{t}{1+t^2}\,dt=0
\end{aligned}\tag{41}$$

This is called the Cauchy principal value and it is zero. But we could also have taken

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle T\rangle &= \lim_{\sigma\to\infty}\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-2\sigma}^{\sigma}\frac{t}{1+t^2}\,dt
\end{aligned}\tag{42}$$

and this is not zero. Thus, depending on how we define the limit, we get different results. That is why we say the mean is undefined.
