Example: The Cauchy distribution

Let $x,y \sim N(0,1)$ iid, and define

$$\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{x}{y}
\end{aligned}\tag{33}$$

We say $T \sim$ Cauchy $(0,1)$. To find the PDF we use Eq (31):

$$\begin{aligned}
P_T(t) &= \int dxdy \ \delta\left(t-\frac{x}{y}\right)P_X(x)P_Y(y)
\end{aligned}\tag{34}$$

we compute the integral over $x$ first. Recall that

$$\begin{aligned}
\delta(a-x) &= \frac{\delta(x)}{|a|}
\end{aligned}\tag{35}$$

then

$$\begin{aligned}
\delta\left(t-\frac{x}{y}\right) &= |y|\delta(ty-x)
\end{aligned}\tag{36}$$
