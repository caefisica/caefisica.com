The bivariate normal

let us practice with the 2-component normal. It's  
covariance matrix is, by definition

$\Theta = \begin{bmatrix} \operatorname{cov}(x_1,x_1) & \operatorname{cov}(x_1,x_2) \\ \operatorname{cov}(x_2,x_1) & \operatorname{cov}(x_2,x_2) \end{bmatrix}$

To simplify the notation let $\sigma_1^2 = \operatorname{cov}(x_1,x_1)$ and $\sigma_2^2 = \operatorname{cov}(x_2,$  
$x_2)$. It is also convenient to define

$\rho = \frac{\operatorname{cov}(x_1,x_2)}{\sigma_1\sigma_2} \in [-1,1]$
