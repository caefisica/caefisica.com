Covariance and independence

If $X,Y$ are independent $\Rightarrow$ $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y) = 0$, But the

converse is in general false: if $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y) = 0$ it does not imply

that $X,Y$ are independent.
