Suppose that the transformation $z=f(x,y)$ and $\omega=g(x,y)$ is one-to-one. Then we may change variables in the integral, from $(x,y)$ to $(z,\omega)$. Recall from calculus that

$$\begin{aligned}
dx\,dy&=dz\,d\omega\left|\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(z,\omega)}\right|
\end{aligned}\tag{43}$$
