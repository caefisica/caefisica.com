If $X_1$ and $X_2$ are independent, $\mathrm{cov}(X_1,X_2)=0$ and we recover our previous result that the variance is additive. When $X_1$ and $X_2$ are not independent, the variance will no longer be additive.

Generalizing (5):

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Var}(X_1+\cdots+X_N)
&=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathrm{Var}(X_i)+2\sum_{i<j}\mathrm{Cov}(X_i,X_j)
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$

In the last term we restrict the sum to $i<j$ and multiply by 2.
