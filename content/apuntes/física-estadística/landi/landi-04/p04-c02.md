Ex: let $z \sim N(0,1)$ and define $x = z$ and $y = z^2$. Thus

are very dependent r.v.s: if you know one, you completely

know the other. Yet

$\operatorname{Cov}(x,y) = \langle xy\rangle - \langle x\rangle \langle y\rangle = \langle z^3\rangle - \langle z\rangle \langle z^2\rangle = 0.$
