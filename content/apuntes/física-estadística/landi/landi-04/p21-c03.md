Here, the 3 components of the velocity are the random  
variables and this is their joint distribution

It is quite clear that the 3 cartesian components are  
statistically independent since

$$\begin{aligned}
p(v_x,v_y,v_z)&=p(v_x)\,p(v_y)\,p(v_z)
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$
