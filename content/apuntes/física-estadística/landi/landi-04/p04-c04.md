If $\operatorname{corr}=0$ they are uncorrelated. If $\operatorname{Corr}>0$ they are positively correlated. In this case $x>0$ makes it more likely that $Y>0$.

If $\operatorname{corr}<0$ they are anti-correlated (or negatively correlated). In this case $x>0$ makes it likely that $Y<0$.
