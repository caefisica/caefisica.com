We will show that $x,y \sim N(0,1)$ iid. To do that we apply Eq. (45). The Jacobian of the transformation is:

$x = \sqrt{-2\ln u}\cos(2\pi v)$  
$y = \sqrt{-2\ln u}\sin(2\pi v)$  
$\rightarrow$  
$u = e^{-(x^2+y^2)/2}$  
$v = \frac{1}{2\pi}\arctan(y/x)$

so

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} &= -x e^{-(x^2+y^2)/2}\\
\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} &= -y e^{-(x^2+y^2)/2}
\end{aligned}\tag{47}$$

we also use the result

$$\frac{d}{dx}\arctan(x)=\frac{1}{1+x^2}\tag{48}$$
