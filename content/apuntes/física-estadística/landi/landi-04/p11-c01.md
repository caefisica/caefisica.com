PDF of the multivariate normal

The PDF of $z = (z_1, \ldots, z_N)$ is easy to find since They  
are independent.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{z_1,\ldots,z_N}(z_1,\ldots,z_N)
&= p_{z_1}(z_1)\cdots p_{z_N}(z_N)\\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^N}}\exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2}(z_1^2+\cdots+z_N^2)\right\}
\end{aligned}
$$

we may write this more concisely as

$$p_z(z) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^N}}\exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2}z^Tz\right\}$$

This is the PDF of $N(0, I_N)$. the PDF of a general Gaussian  
$N(\mu, \Theta)$ is

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{p_x(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^N\det(\Theta)}}\exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2}(x-\mu)^T\Theta^{-1}(x-\mu)\right\}}
\end{aligned}\tag{29}$$

I will demonstrate this formula at the end of these notes  
(I will not do it in class).

when $\mu=0$ and $\Theta=I_N$ we recover the PDF of $z$.
