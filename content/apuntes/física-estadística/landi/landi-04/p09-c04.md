$$\begin{aligned}
\langle xx^{T}\rangle &= Q\Theta Q^{T}+\mu\mu^{T}
\end{aligned}\tag{26}$$

This is not yet $\Theta(x)$, Eq (18). To get the actual covariance matrix we must subtract $\langle x\rangle\langle x\rangle^{T}=\mu\mu^{T}$. We then get
