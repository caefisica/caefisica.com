The Gibbs probability distribution will then be

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x,y,z,p_x,p_y,p_z) &= \frac{e^{-\beta(p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2)/2m}}{V(2\pi m k_B T)^{3/2}}
\end{aligned}\tag{32}$$

Notice that the probabilities for the position and momenta factor as a product:

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x,y,z,p_x,p_y,p_z) &= P(x,y,z)\,\Omega(p_x,p_y,p_z)
\end{aligned}\tag{33}$$
