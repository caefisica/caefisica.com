The student who studied Thermodynamics may recall that  
the pressure is related to the free energy according to

$P = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial V}$

$$\begin{aligned}
P &= -Nk_BT\frac{\partial Q}{\partial V} \quad (\ln V + \cdots)\\
&= \frac{Nk_BT}{V}
\end{aligned}$$
