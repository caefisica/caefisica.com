A gas of interacting particles

Suppose we have N molecules in a box, the general Hamiltonian describing these particles and their interactions is

$$\begin{aligned}
H &= \sum_{k=1}^{N} \frac{p_k^2}{2m} + \sum_{i,j<i} V(r_i,r_j)
\end{aligned}\tag{69}$$
