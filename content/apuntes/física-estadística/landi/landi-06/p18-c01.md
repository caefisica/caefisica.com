The heat capacity and the specific heat

Another quantity of great importance in thermodynamics  
and statistical mechanics is the heat capacity. It is defined  
as

$$\begin{aligned}
C &= \frac{\partial U}{\partial T}
\end{aligned}\tag{51}$$

It represents the sensitivity of U to changes in T. For the  
ideal gas $U = \frac{3}{2}Nk_BT$ [Eq (46)] so

$$\begin{aligned}
C &= \frac{3}{2}Nk_B
\end{aligned}\tag{52}$$
