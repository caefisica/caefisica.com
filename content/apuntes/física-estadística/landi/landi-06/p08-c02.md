$$\begin{aligned}
P(y)&=\frac{e^{\alpha H(y)}}{Z}
\end{aligned}\tag{18}$$

where $Z$ and $\alpha$ are constants to be determined.

$Z$ is easy: it is just a normalization constant. As for $\alpha$, here is what we can say about it: consider two microstates $y$ and $y'$ and let us look at

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{P(y')}{P(y)}&=\frac{e^{\alpha H(y')}}{e^{\alpha H(y)}}=e^{\alpha[H(y')-H(y)]}
\end{aligned}\tag{19}$$
