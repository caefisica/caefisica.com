when a system is composed of many independent parts,  
H will be a sum of terms of the form $H = H_1 + \cdots + H_N$. Each  
$H_i$ is a r.v. and if the subparts do not interact, they will  
be independent. thus, according to the central limit  
theorem, $H$ will be approximately given by a Normal  
distribution. Its mean is $\langle H\rangle = 0$ and, according  
to (9.0), its variance will be  

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Var}(H) = \langle H^2\rangle - \langle H\rangle^2 = c k_B T
\end{aligned}\tag{1.8}$$
