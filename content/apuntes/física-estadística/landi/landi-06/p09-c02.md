But what can we say about $\beta$. Thus far everything has been a theoretical construct. Now experiment must come in. We need to compare our theory with known experimental results. (we will do that along this course). In doing so we will conclude that

$$\begin{aligned}
\beta = \frac{1}{k_B T}
\end{aligned}\tag{22}$$

where $T$ is the temperature of the bath and $k_B$ is the Boltzmann constant

$$\begin{aligned}
k_B &= 1.380648 \times 10^{-23}\ \text{J/K}\\
&= 8.617332 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{eV/K}
\end{aligned}\tag{23}$$
