$$\boxed{\begin{aligned}
g &= \text{microstate} = \text{specification of all coordinate}\\
&\quad \text{and momenta}
\end{aligned}}\tag{1}$$

If your system is composed of a single particle, then

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Single particle:}\quad g &= (x_1,x_2,x_3,p_1,p_2,p_3)
\end{aligned}\tag{2}$$

If you have $N$ particles then

$$\begin{aligned}
N\text{ particles:}\quad g &= (x_1,\ldots,x_{3N},p_1,\ldots,p_{3N})
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$
