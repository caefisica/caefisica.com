$H=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{p_i^2}{2m}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{(p_{xi}^2+p_{yi}^2+p_{zi}^2)}{2m}$

Let us now compute the partition function. To practice, suppose first that $N=2$ (only 2 particles in the box), then

$$\begin{aligned}
Z_2&=\int e^{-\beta H}\,d^3r_1\,d^3r_2\,d^3p_1\,d^3p_2\\
&=\int e^{-\beta H_1}\,d^3r_1\,d^3p_1\int e^{-\beta H_2}\,d^3r_2\,d^3p_2
\end{aligned}\tag{43}$$

I knew this looks ugly. But I wanted you to see that, since the particles do not interact, the partition function will simply be a product of the individual partition functions. Please remember this. It may save your life one day
