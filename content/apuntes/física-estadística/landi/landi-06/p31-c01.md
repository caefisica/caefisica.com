Suppose our gas is trapped in a cylinder with a movable  
piston. If we compress the piston by our amount $\Delta x$,  
the change in energy (mechanical work) will  
be

$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta E &= F\,\Delta x = pA\,\Delta x
\end{aligned}\tag{87}$$

where $A$ = cross section area and $p$ is the pressure. Now, $A\Delta x=-\Delta V$  
is the change in volume (the negative sign is because you compressed the cylinder). Thus $\Delta E=-p\Delta V$, or

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{pressure} &= -\frac{\partial H}{\partial V}
\end{aligned}\tag{88}$$
