The constant $Z$ is called the partition function. It is used  
to ensure that the probabilities $P(y)$ are correctly norma-  
lized:

$$\begin{aligned}
\int P(y)\,dx_1\ldots dx_{3N}\,dp_1\ldots dp_{3N} &= 1
\end{aligned}\tag{25}$$

We must then have

$$\frac{1}{Z}\int e^{-\beta H(y)}\,dx_1\ldots dx_{3N}\,dp_1\ldots dp_{3N}=1$$
