$$\begin{aligned}
P(p_x,p_y,p_z)&=\frac{1}{(2\pi m k_BT)^{3/2}}\exp\left\{-\frac{\beta}{2m}(p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2)\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{73}$$

Maxwell and Boltzmann were very interested in this formula  
because it describes the distribution of momenta in a gas  
(they derived these formulae before Gibbs' theory, using other  
methods).

Actually, they preferred to work with velocities: $v_x=\frac{p_x}{m}$.

Since this is a 1-to-1 transformation we may use the formula

$\rho(v_x,v_y,v_z)=P(p_x,p_y,p_z)\left|\frac{\partial(p_x,p_y,p_z)}{\partial(v_x,v_y,v_z)}\right|$
