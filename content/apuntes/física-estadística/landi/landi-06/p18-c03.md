the heat capacity per particle is called the specific heat

$$\begin{aligned}
C &= \frac{\partial U}{\partial T} = \text{heat capacity}\\
c &= \frac{C}{N} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial T} = \text{specific heat}
\end{aligned}\tag{54}$$
