Most books introduce the canonical ensemble using  
a different route than the one we will use here. They  
start with a theory called the microcanonical ensemble.  
We will study this theory later on in the course.

The derivation of the canonical ensemble, as is done here,  
may also be found in:

• Feynman, Statistical Mechanics; a set of lectures, chap 1  
• Landau, Statistical Physics, chap 1.
