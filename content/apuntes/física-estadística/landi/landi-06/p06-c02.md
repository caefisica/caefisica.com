Heat bath

The first part has microstate $y_1$ and energy $H_1(y_1)$, whereas the second part has microstate $y_2$ and energy $H_2(y_2)$. If we think of both system as just one single system, then its microstate will be $y=(y_1,y_2)$. Moreover since the two systems do not interact, the total energy will be simply the sum of the individual energies

$$\begin{aligned}
H(y) &= H_1(y_1) + H_2(y_2)
\end{aligned}\tag{10}$$
