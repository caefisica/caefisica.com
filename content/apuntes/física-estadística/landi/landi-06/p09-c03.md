$k_B$ is a constant which converts from kelvin to energy units such as J or eV. then $[k_B T]$ = energy and $\beta$ is dimensionless.

In summary, the equilibrium probabilities are

$$\begin{aligned}
P(y) &= \frac{e^{-\beta H(y)}}{Z}, \quad \beta = \frac{1}{k_B T}
\end{aligned}\tag{24}$$
