Example: $3N$ harmonic oscillators

Later on we will study the specific heat of solids.

We will learn that, to a certain approximation, the solid may be modeled as a collection of $3N$ independent harmonic oscillators, with Hamiltonian

$$\begin{aligned}
H &= \sum_{i=1}^{3N} H_i = \sum_{i=1}^{3N} \left[\frac{p_i^2}{2m} + \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x_i^2\right]
\end{aligned}\tag{55}$$
