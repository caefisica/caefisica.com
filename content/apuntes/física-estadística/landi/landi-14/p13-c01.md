Using Eq (10) we may also write the DOS in a more,  
convenient way:

$N=\frac{V}{2\pi^2}\left(\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}\frac{2}{3}E_F^{3/2}$

and

$D(e)=\frac{V}{2\pi^2}\left(\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}\sqrt{e}$

Thus

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{D(e)=\frac{3N}{2E_F^{3/2}}\sqrt{e}}&\Rightarrow
\boxed{D(E_F)=\frac{3N}{2E_F}}
\end{aligned}\tag{11}$$

Now let's compute the total energy of the system:

$$\begin{aligned}
U&=\int_0^{E_F}de\,D(e)\,e
\end{aligned}\tag{12}$$

this is the internal energy at zero temperature. we have

$U=\frac{3N}{2E_F^{3/2}}\int_0^{E_F}de\,e^{3/2}=\frac{3N}{2E_F^{3/2}}\frac{2}{5}E_F^{5/2}$
