With the DOS we may now compute the Fermi energy as

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^{\epsilon_F} d\epsilon\,D(\epsilon)&=N
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

Let's check:

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^{\epsilon_F} d\epsilon\,D(\epsilon)
&=\frac{V}{2\pi^2}\left(\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}
\int_0^{\epsilon_F}d\epsilon\,\sqrt{\epsilon}\\
&=\frac{V}{2\pi^2}\left(\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}
\frac{2}{3}\epsilon_F^{3/2}\\
&=N
\end{aligned}\tag{10}$$

Thus

$\epsilon_F^{3/2}=3\pi^2N/V\left(\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\right)^{3/2}$

or

$\epsilon_F=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left(3\pi^2N/V\right)^{2/3}$
