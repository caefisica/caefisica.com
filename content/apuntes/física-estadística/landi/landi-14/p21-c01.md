what we need to be careful about are the integrals. we  
may write

$\int_0^{E_F} f(\epsilon)d\epsilon = \int_0^\mu f(\epsilon)d\epsilon + \int_\mu^{E_F} f(\epsilon)d\epsilon$

we assume $E_F$ and $\mu$ are close to each other so we may  
approximate

$\int_\mu^{E_F} f(\epsilon)d\epsilon \simeq (E_F-\mu)f\left(\frac{E_F+\mu}{2}\right)$

where the function was evaluated midway through the interval.

But

$f\left(\frac{E_F+\mu}{2}\right) = f\left(E_F-\frac{\alpha}{2}(k_BT)^2\right) \simeq f(E_F)-\frac{\alpha}{2}(k_BT)^2f'(E_F)$

Retaining only terms. up to order $(k_BT)^2$ we then get

$\int_\mu^{E_F} f(\epsilon)d\epsilon \simeq (E_F-\mu)f'(E_F)-\underbrace{\underbrace{(E_F-\mu)}_{\sim(k_BT)^2}\frac{\alpha}{2}(k_BT)^2}_{\sim(k_BT)^4}f'(E_F)$
