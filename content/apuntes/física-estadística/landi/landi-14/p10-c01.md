Using (2.29) to evaluate (2.27), we find that the energy density of the electron gas is:

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{E}{V} &= \frac{1}{4\pi^3}\int_{k<k_F} dk\,\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m}
= \frac{1}{\pi^2}\frac{\hbar^2k_F^5}{10m}.
\end{aligned}\tag{2.30}$$

To find the energy per electron, $E/N$, in the ground state, we must divide this by  
$N/V = k_F^3/3\pi^2$, which gives

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{E}{N} &= \frac{3}{10}\frac{\hbar^2k_F^2}{m}
= \frac{3}{5}\epsilon_F.
\end{aligned}\tag{2.31}$$
