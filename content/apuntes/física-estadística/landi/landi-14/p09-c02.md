$$\begin{aligned}
k_B T_F &= E_F
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$

this gives a rough measure of the temperature at which the electron gas would behave classically. For all metals, $T_F \sim 10^4 K$, meaning that only at 10000 K they would behave classically. But this is way above the melting point of the solid. conclusion; electrons in a metal must always be treated quantum mechanically. Always.
