Thus

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{U=\frac{3}{5}NE_F}
\end{aligned}\tag{13}$$

At $T=0$ the average energy of each electron is $\frac{3}{5}E_F$.

Naively we could think that it should be $\frac{1}{2}E_F$ since all states up to $E_F$ are filled. But the density of states is not uniform; it increase as $\sqrt{E}$, meaning that there are more states with higher energy. Consequently, when we average we get $\frac{3}{5}=0.6$ instead of $0.5$.
