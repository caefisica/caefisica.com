Thus, the volume of each cube is

$\Delta k_x \Delta k_y \Delta k_z = \left(\frac{2\pi}{L}\right)^3$.

If there are $N$ electrons, then we can fill 2 electrons per cube  
(because of spin $\sigma = \pm 1$), thus, we will occupy in total $N/2$ cubes,

which implies a volume

$\frac{N}{2}\left(\frac{2\pi}{L}\right)^3 = \frac{N}{V}\frac{(2\pi)^3}{2}$
