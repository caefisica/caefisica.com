Solving for $\mu$:

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\mu=E_F-\frac{\pi^2}{6}(k_BT)^2\frac{D'(E_F)}{D(E_F)}}
\end{aligned}\tag{36}$$

This shows, as anticipated, that the first correction to $\mu$ is of the order $(k_BT)^2$.

Now we look at (35). Recall that, from Eq (13)

$\displaystyle \int_0^{E_F}D(\epsilon)\epsilon\,d\epsilon=\frac{3}{5}NE_F$

We then get

$\displaystyle U=\frac{3}{5}NE_F+E_F\overbrace{\left\{(\mu-E_F)D(E_F)+\frac{\pi^2}{6}(k_BT)^2D'(E_F)\right\}}^{=0\text{ because of (36)}}+\frac{\pi^2}{6}(k_BT)^2D(E_F)$
