Thus we finally conclude that

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{U = \frac{3}{5}NE_F + \frac{\pi^2}{6}(k_B T)^2D(E_F).}
\end{aligned}\tag{37}$$

the heat capacity is

$$\begin{aligned}
C = \frac{\partial U}{\partial T} - \frac{\pi^2}{3}k_B^2TD(E_F)
\end{aligned}\tag{38}$$

so the specific heat will be

$$\begin{aligned}
c = \frac{C}{N} = \frac{\pi^2k_B^2}{3}T\frac{D(E_F)}{N}
\end{aligned}\tag{39}$$

From (11) we had that $D(E_F) = \frac{3N}{2E_F}$. Thus

$$c = \frac{\pi^2k_B^2T}{3}\frac{3}{2}\frac{N}{E_F}\frac{1}{N}$$
