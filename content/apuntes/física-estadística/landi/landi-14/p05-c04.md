The number $N$ of electrons is actually determined by the type of atom in your metal. For instance, Au has a valency $z=1$, meaning that each Au atom contributes $1$ electron to the gas. The other electrons in the Au atom remain bounded to the nucleus. Combining this with the density of Au, we then obtain an electron density of $n = 5.9 \times 10^{22}$ electrons /cm$^3$. that is, a crystal of $1$ cm$^3$ volume will have $N = 5.9 \times 10^{22}$ electrons, which will gradually fill all energy states, as discussed.
