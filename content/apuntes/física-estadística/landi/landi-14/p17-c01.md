It is clear from this analysis that

$$\begin{aligned}
\mu(T=0) &= \epsilon_F
\end{aligned}\tag{21}$$

For $T\ne0$ the chemical potential will change, but at $T=0$ it is  
simply the Fermi energy.

we may also write the Fermi-Dirac distribution as

$$\begin{aligned}
\bar{n}(\epsilon) &= \Theta(\epsilon_F-\epsilon) \qquad (T=0)
\end{aligned}\tag{22}$$

where $\Theta(x)$ is called the step function, or Heaviside function

$$\begin{aligned}
\Theta(x) &= \begin{cases}
1 & x>0\\
0 & x<0
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\tag{23}$$
