The ground state of a free electron gas

By far, the most important type of Fermi gas is a metal

In a metal, the electrons are weakly bound to the ions and  
may therefore move almost freely through the crystal. Their only  
constraint is that they cannot leave the metal. There is a potential  
barrier for escaping (this is the work function that you study  
in the context of the photoelectric effect).

Thus, to a first approximation, we may treat electrons  
in a metal as simply particles in a box.
