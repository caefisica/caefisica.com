Metal.

If the electron is ever excited to a sufficiently high energy  
state, it may eventually be removed from the box, as in the  
photoelectric effect. But if we assume no such processes take place,  
then we may assume the potential well is infinite. This is what  
we will do here.

Since electrons are Fermions we therefore reach the  
following conclusion:

"Electrons in a metal $\approx$ a Fermigas in a box"
