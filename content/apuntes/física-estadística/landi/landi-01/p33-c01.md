Conditional probability

Let $X$ and $Y$ be two (not necessarily independent) r.v.s.

we define the conditional probability as

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x=k \mid y=l) &= \text{Prob. of } X=k \text{ given that } Y=l
\end{aligned}\tag{5.5}$$
