The Poisson distribution

The Poisson distribution is characterised by a single parameter $\lambda$ and has the distribution

$$\begin{aligned} x \sim \mathrm{Poiss}(\lambda); \qquad P_k &= e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}, \qquad k=0,1,2,\ldots \end{aligned}\tag{24}$$
