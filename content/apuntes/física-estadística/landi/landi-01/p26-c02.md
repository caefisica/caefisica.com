$\frac{\partial}{\partial \lambda}: \quad e^\lambda=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k\lambda^{k-1}}{k!}$

Representing $\lambda$:

$\lambda e^\lambda=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k\lambda^k}{k!}$

$\frac{\partial}{\partial \lambda}: \quad (1+\lambda)e^\lambda=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k^2\lambda^{k-1}}{k!}$

Representing again:

$\lambda(1+\lambda)e^\lambda=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k^2\lambda^k}{k!}$
