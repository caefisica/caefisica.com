The Bernoulli distribution

The simplest example of a discrete r.v. is one which may take on the values $x=0$ or $x=1$, the probability then may then be parametrized as

$$\begin{aligned}
P_1 &= P(x=1)=p && p\in[0,1]\\
P_0 &= P(x=0)=q=1-p
\end{aligned}\tag{5}$$

The distribution is completely characterized by the parameter $p$. In the statistics literature it is common to write

$$\begin{aligned}
X &\sim \operatorname{Bern}(p)
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$
