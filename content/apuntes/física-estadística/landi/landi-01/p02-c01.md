Probability theory and statistical mechanics

I find it quite amazing that the exact same theory we  
use to analyse data from the lab experiment is also used  
in completely uncorrelated areas, such as medical trials or  
opinion polls. There is, of course, a reason for this: probability  
theory is what we could call a "stem theory", in analogy  
with stem cells, which adapt to the task at hand.

of rolling a dice, there are 6 possible outcomes
