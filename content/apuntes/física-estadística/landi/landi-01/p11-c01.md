The normalization relation of $P_k(1)$ may be checked using the  
Binomial theorem

$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k=0}^{n} P_k &= \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} q^{n-k} p^k = (q+p)^n = 1^n = 1
\end{aligned}\tag{15}$$

Ex : Exponential model
