Remember this:

If two quantities are independent, the prob. is a product. (4.3)

Statistical independence implies the following:

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle xy\rangle &= \sum_{k,\ell} k\ell\,P(x=k,y=\ell)\\
&= \sum_{k,\ell} k\ell\,P(x=k)P(y=\ell)\\
&= \left[\sum_k k\,P(x=k)\right]\left[\sum_\ell \ell\,P(y=\ell)\right]\\
&= \langle x\rangle\langle y\rangle
\end{aligned}$$
