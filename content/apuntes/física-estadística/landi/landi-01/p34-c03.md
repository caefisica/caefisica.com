\end{aligned}\tag{58}$$

we condition $Y = k$ on all possible outcomes of $X_1$ and sum  
over the result. But since $X_1$ and $X_2$ are independent

$P(Y = k \mid X_1 = \ell) = P(X_1 + X_2 = k \mid X_1 = \ell)$

$= P(X_2 = k - \ell)$ $\leftarrow$ I used independence.
