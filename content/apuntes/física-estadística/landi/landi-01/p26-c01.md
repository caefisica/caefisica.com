Example: variance of the Paisson

we first compute $\langle x^2\rangle$ using the differentiation trick:

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle x^2\rangle &= e^{-\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} k^2\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}
\end{aligned}\tag{3.8}$$

we have:

$e^\lambda=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}$
