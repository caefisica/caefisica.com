If $p = 1/2$ (simple random walk) we get

$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(Y_t) &= t
\end{aligned}\tag{54}$$

So even though on average you are not going anywhere,  
the width of your distribution increases with the number  
of steps.
