Example: $X \sim Bern(p)$

Recall we compute

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle X^2\rangle &= (0)^2p_0 + (1)^2p_1 = p
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$

In fact, for Bern(p)

$$\begin{aligned}
\langle X^n\rangle &= p,\quad n=1,2,3,\ldots
\end{aligned}\tag{3'}$$
