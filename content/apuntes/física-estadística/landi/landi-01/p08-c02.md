the symbol “$\sim$” means “is distributed according to”. $X$ is a r.v. It does not have a specific value. So all we can do is specify how it is distributed.

The Bernoulli distribution appears often in physics as representing the occupation of some site, when $x=0$ we say the site is empty and when $x=1$ we say it is occupied.

We also have use may use $\operatorname{Bern}(p)$ to represent yes/no events. For instance, we may associate

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{when he is something} &: x=1\\
\text{when he is not something} &: x=0.
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$
