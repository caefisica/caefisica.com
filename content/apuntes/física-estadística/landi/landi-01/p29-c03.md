Recall: let

$$\begin{aligned}
x_i &\sim Bern(p), \quad i=1,\ldots,m
\end{aligned}\tag{47}$$

we say that the $x_i$ are independently, identically distributed

(iid) r.v.s. then

$$\begin{aligned}
y &= x_1+\cdots+x_m \sim Bin(m,p)
\end{aligned}\tag{48}$$
