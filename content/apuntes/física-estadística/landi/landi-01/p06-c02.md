The statement "winter is coming" is the event.

How use will be interested in the situation where the event  
is a number, that is, we have some quantity $x$ which may  
take on a series of values with certain probabilities. this  
quantity $x$ is called a random variable (r.v.). For now  
we will assume that the values taken on by $x$ are discrete.  
the continuous case will be treated in the next set of notes.

To each value $k$ that $x$ may take, we have an associated  
probability

$$\begin{aligned}
P_k &= P(x = k) = \text{prob. that }x\text{ takes on the value }k
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$
