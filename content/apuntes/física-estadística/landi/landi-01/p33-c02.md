Mathematically, we define it from

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x=k, y=l) &= P(x=k \mid y=l)P(y=l)\\
&= P(y=l \mid x=k)P(x=k)
\end{aligned}\tag{56}$$

Please take a second to think about the motivation behind this formula.

In terms of conditional probabilities, we may also formulate the definition of statistical independence in our intuitive way:

$$\begin{aligned}
P(x=k \mid y=l) &= P(x=k)
\end{aligned}\tag{57}$$
