$\operatorname{Var}(z)=\langle z^2\rangle-\langle z\rangle^2$

$=\left[\langle x^2\rangle+2\langle x\rangle\langle y\rangle+\langle y^2\rangle\right]-\left[\langle x\rangle^2+2\langle x\rangle\langle y\rangle+\langle y\rangle^2\right]$

$=\langle x^2\rangle-\langle x\rangle^2+\langle y^2\rangle-\langle y\rangle^2$
