"According to prob. theory, the prob. of obtaining each outcome is $1/6$"

But that is just true! "prob. theory" never said that, that is an assumption that you made: you assumed that the 6 outcomes were equally likely so, since the probabilities must add up to $1$, each must be $1/6$.

The reason why prob. theory is a "stem theory" and the reason why it is so successful is because it never attributes probabilities to events. that part is up to you. What the theory does is it tells you how to compute relevant quantities once the probabilities are given.
