$$\begin{aligned}
P(Y = k) &= \sum_{\ell=0}^{k} P(X_2 = k-\ell)P(X_1 = \ell)\\
&= e^{-\lambda}e^{-\lambda}\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\frac{\lambda^{k-\ell}}{(k-\ell)!}\frac{\lambda^\ell}{\ell!}\\
&= e^{-2\lambda}\lambda^k\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\frac{1}{\ell!(k-\ell)!}\\
&= e^{-2\lambda}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}\underbrace{\sum_{\ell=0}^{k}\binom{k}{\ell}}_{=2^k\text{ by the Binomial theorem}}\\
&= e^{-2\lambda}\frac{(2\lambda)^k}{k!}

Thus we see that, indeed, $Y \sim \operatorname{Pois}(2\lambda)$.
