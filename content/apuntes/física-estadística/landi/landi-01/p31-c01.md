Random walk

Suppose you are drunk and you are trying to get home  
from a party. At each time you either take a step to the  
left or to the right. Assume also that the steps always have  
the same length, which we define as "1" in some weird  
discrete sequence of events, then each step may be described  
by a random variable $X_i = \pm 1$.

After t step your position will be

$$\begin{aligned}
y_t &= x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_t,
\end{aligned}\tag{50}$$
