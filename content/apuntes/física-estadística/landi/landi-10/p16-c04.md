Let $E = E_1 + E_2$. the composite system is isolated, but the individual parts will exchange heat with each other until they reach thermal equilibrium. According to the 2nd law, the equilibrium state will be that which maximizes the multiplicity

$$\begin{aligned}
\Omega(E, E_1) &= \Omega_1(E_1)\Omega_2(E-E_1)
\end{aligned}\tag{25}$$

Here I noted that $E$ is fixed (because the system is isolated) so $\Omega$ will actually only depend on $E_1$ and we may write

$E_2 = E - E_1$.
