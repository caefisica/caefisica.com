Spin $\frac{1}{2}$ paramagnetism revisited

Let us consider once again the problem of spin $\frac{1}{2}$ paramagnetism  
we N particles, each described by a variable $\sigma_i = \pm 1$, $i = 1,\ldots,N$.  
The total energy of interaction between the spins and a magnetic  
field $B$ is

$$\begin{aligned}
E &= -\mu B \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sigma_i
\end{aligned}\tag{46}$$
