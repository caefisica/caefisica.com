Example: 2-state system

In (17) we found that

$\lambda = \frac{S}{N} = -k_B \ln(1-\mu) - k_B\mu \ln\left(\frac{\mu}{1-\mu}\right)$

where $\mu = E/N\epsilon$. Now;

$\frac{\partial S}{\partial E} = \frac{\partial(S/N)}{\partial(E/N)} = \frac{1}{\epsilon}\frac{\partial\lambda}{\partial\mu}$

$= -\frac{k_B}{\epsilon}\frac{\partial}{\partial\mu}\ln(1-\mu) - \frac{k_B}{\epsilon}\frac{\partial}{\partial\mu}\left[\mu\ln\left(\frac{\mu}{1-\mu}\right)\right]$
