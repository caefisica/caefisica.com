We have therefore reduced the problem to a combinatorical

question; how many ways are there to choose $N_1$ out of $N$ particles ?

the answer is

$$\begin{aligned}
\Omega(E) &= \binom{N}{N_1} = \frac{N!}{N_1!(N-N_1)!}
\end{aligned}\tag{11}$$
