The “macrostate” here means a given energy $E$.

Example: two - state system

Suppose you have $N$ particles that can either have energy $E_0 = 0$ or $E_1 = \epsilon$. How many ways are there to configure the system so that the total energy is $E$? That is $\Omega(E)$.

Now, let $N_1$ be the number of particles with energy $E_1 = \epsilon$ and $N_0 = N - N_1$ the number of particles with energy $E_0 = 0$. Then the allowed energies $E$ of the system will be multiples of $\epsilon$ since

$$\begin{aligned}
E &= N_0 E_0 + N_1 E_1 = N_1 \epsilon
\end{aligned}\tag{10}$$
