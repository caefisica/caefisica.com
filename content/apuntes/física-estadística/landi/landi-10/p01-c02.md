Canonical Ensemble (Gibbs formula, $P = \frac{e^{-\beta E}}{Z}$);

↳ Your system can have any size, but it is always coupled  
to a very large 'heat bath.

Microcanonical Ensemble ($S = \ln \Omega$)

↳ Your system itself is very large and it is completely isolated

The microcanonical ensemble is valuable from a conceptual  
point of view: it will help us have a deeper interpretation of thermal  
phenomena. However, it is terrible for solving problems.
