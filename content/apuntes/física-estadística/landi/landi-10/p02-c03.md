Since we are talking about a maximum. $f'(x_0)=0$ and $f''(x_0)<0$.

Eq (1) then becomes

$$\begin{aligned}
I &\simeq \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} dx\,\exp\left\{N\left[f(x_0)-\frac{|f''(x_0)|}{2!}(x-x_0)^2\right]\right\}\\
&=e^{Nf(x_0)}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}dx\,e^{-a(x-x_0)^2}
\qquad a=\frac{N|f''(x_0)|}{2}
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
