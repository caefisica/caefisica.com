$S = S_1(E_1, V_1) + S_2(E_2, V_2)$

$= S_1(E_1, V_1) + S_2(E - E_1, V - V_1)$

According to the 2nd law, the equilibrium configuration will  
be that which maximizes $S$. Thus we should set

$\frac{\partial S}{\partial E_1} = 0 \qquad \frac{\partial S}{\partial V_1} = 0$
