$P_m = c\frac{\Omega(E-E_m)}{\Omega(E)} = c\exp\{\ln\Omega(E-E_m)-\ln\Omega(E)\}$

$=c\exp\left\{\frac{S(E-E_m)-S(E)}{k_B}\right\}$
