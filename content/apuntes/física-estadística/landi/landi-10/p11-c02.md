$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
P_{S+R} & \simeq & P_R P_S \leftarrow P_m\\
\uparrow & & \uparrow\\
\dfrac{1}{\Omega(E)} & & \dfrac{1}{\Omega_R(E-E_m)}
\end{array}
$$

Since the total energy is $E$, and since we are asking about $S$ having  
energy $E_m$, the reservoir energy must be $E-E_m$. So its probability  
will be proportional to $\Omega_R(E-E_m)$, where $\Omega_R$ is the multiplicity of  
the reservoir.

Thus

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_m &= \frac{\Omega_R(E-E_m)}{\Omega(E)}
\end{aligned}\tag{19}
$$
