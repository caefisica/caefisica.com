$Z_1=\sum_{m=0,1}e^{-\beta E_m}=e^{-\beta E_0}+e^{-\beta E_1}=1+e^{-\beta\epsilon}$

$\omega_1=-\frac{\partial}{\partial\beta}\ln Z_1=-\frac{1}{1+e^{-\beta\epsilon}}\left(-\epsilon e^{-\beta\epsilon}\right)$
