$$\begin{aligned}
z &= c\int dE\,\exp\left\{\frac{S(E)}{k_B}-\beta E\right\}
\end{aligned}\tag{31}$$

Now, both $S$ and $E$ are extensive. So this has the form

$\frac{S-\beta E}{k_B}=N\left(\frac{s}{k_B}-\beta\epsilon\right)$

which falls precisely onto the framework of Laplace's method.

The $1^{st}$ step is therefore to maximise $S-\beta E$ with respect to $E$:

$\frac{\partial}{\partial E}\left[\frac{S(E)}{k_B}-\beta E\right]=\left.\frac{1}{k_B}\frac{\partial S}{\partial E}\right|_{E=E^*}-\beta=0$

This therefore defines an energy $E^*$; which maximizes $S-\beta E$.

This energy will be a function of $\beta$: $E^*(\beta)$.

$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\left.\frac{1}{k_B}\frac{\partial S}{\partial E}\right|_{E=E^*}=\beta\\
\text{Defines }E^*(\beta)
\end{gathered}}
\end{aligned}\tag{32}$$
