$$\begin{aligned}
N! &\equiv \int_0^\infty dx\,x^N e^{-x}
= \int_0^\infty dx\,e^{-x+N\ln x}
\end{aligned}\tag{5}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
&= \int_0^\infty dx\,\exp\{N(\ln x-x/N)\}
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$

this is the form (3) with $f(x)=\ln x-x/N$. This function will

have a maximum at

$0=f'(x)=\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{N}\Longrightarrow x_0=N$

Thus, applying (3) we get $f(x_0)=\ln N-1$, $\left|f''(x_0)\right|=\frac{1}{N^2}$

$$\begin{aligned}
N! &\simeq (2\pi N)^{1/2}e^{N(\ln N-1)}
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\ln N! &\simeq N\ln N-N+\frac{1}{2}\ln(2\pi N)
\end{aligned}\tag{8}$$

The last term is $O(\ln N)$ so for large $N$ it becomes completely irrelevant. Eq (8) is Stirling's formula.
