There are $2^N$ possible configurations of the $\sigma_i$, but the number  
of distinct energy levels is not so high.

In fact, if we let $N_+$ be the number of particles with spin  
up and $N_-$ the number of particles with spin down, then we  
may write

$$\begin{aligned}
E &= -\mu B N_+ + \mu B N_-
\end{aligned}\tag{57}$$

But $N_+ + N_- = N$ so the energy depends only on $N_+$ (or $N_-$);

$E = -\mu B N_+ + \mu B (N - N_+)$
