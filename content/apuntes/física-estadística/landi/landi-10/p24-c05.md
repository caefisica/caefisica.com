We now use Stirling's formula

$\ln N! \simeq N\ln N-N \longrightarrow N! = e^{N(\ln N-1)} = e^{N\ln(N/e)}$

$= \left(\frac{N}{e}\right)^N$

Thus we get

$$\begin{aligned}
\Gamma\left(\frac{3N}{2}\right) &\simeq \left(\frac{3N}{2e}\right)^{\frac{3N}{2}}
\end{aligned}\tag{41}$$
