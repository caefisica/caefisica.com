we have constructed thermodynamics by associating $Z$ to  
the free energy $F = - k_B T \ln Z$.

But, in practice, $P(y_1, \ldots, y_N)$ is not experimentally accessible.

$P(y_1, y_2, y_3) =$ Prob that particle 1 is at $y_1$, particle 2 is at $y_2$  
and particle 3 is at $y_3$.

what we can actually know is

Prob. one particle is at $y_1$, another at $y_2$ and another at $y_3$.
