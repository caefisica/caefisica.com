$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{p}\psi_k(x) &= \hbar k\psi_k(x), \qquad \psi_k(x)=e^{ikx}
\end{aligned}\tag{10}$$

The eigenvalues of $\hat{p}$ are $\hbar k$, where $k$ can be any real number. We have a continuum of eigenvalues. I know that may sound weird at first, but there is nothing wrong about it.

The quantity $k$ has units of $1/\text{length}$. we say it has units of \underline{wave-number} or \underline{wave-vector} then $\hbar k$ has units of momentum.
