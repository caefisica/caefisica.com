This has precisely the structure of a Riemann sum, so

when $N$ is large we may approximate

$N\sum_{m=1}^{N}\Delta x\ln x_m \approx N\int_{0}^{1}\ln x\,dx$

the limits of integration are $m=1 \Rightarrow x_m=1/N\approx 0$ and  
$m=N \Rightarrow x_m=1$, this integral can be solved by integration
