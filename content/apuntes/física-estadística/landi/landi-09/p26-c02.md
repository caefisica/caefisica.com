Since the vibrations do not contribute, the main contri-
bution to c must come from the rotations. the energy  
of rotations of a system with angular momentum L is

$$\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{rot}} &= \frac{L^2}{2I}
\end{aligned}\tag{50}$$

where I is the moment of inertia
