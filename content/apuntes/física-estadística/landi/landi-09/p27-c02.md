In quantum mechanics we learn that the eigenvalues of the  
angular momentum operator $\hat{L}^2$ are

$\mathrm{eigs}(\hat{L}^2) = \hbar^2 \ell(\ell+1), \quad \ell=0,1,2,\ldots$

Thus, Eq (50) must be replaced by

$E_{\mathrm{rot}} = \frac{\hbar^2}{2I}\ell(\ell+1)$
