$$\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\psi_{\vec{k}}(\vec{x})=\frac{1}{\sqrt{V}}e^{i\vec{k}\cdot\vec{x}}}
\end{aligned}\tag{30}$$

where $V=L^3$ in the volume of the box. This function satisfies

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{p}_i\psi_{\vec{k}}(\vec{x})=\hbar k_i\psi_{\vec{k}}(\vec{x}),\quad i=x,y,z
\end{aligned}\tag{31}$$

Moreover

$$\begin{aligned}
k_i=\frac{2\pi}{L}m_i,\quad m_i=0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\ldots
\end{aligned}\tag{32}$$

so we have independent integers $m_x$, $m_y$, $m_z$ for each direction.

Finally

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{H}\psi_{\vec{k}}=E_k\psi_{\vec{k}}
\end{aligned}$$
