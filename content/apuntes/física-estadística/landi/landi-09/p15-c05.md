when $L$ is large, $\Delta u_x$ will be tiny. So we may  
multiply the sum by $1 = \frac{L}{2\pi}\Delta u_x$ and write

$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k_x} e^{-\beta\hbar^2 k_x^2/2m}
&= \frac{L}{2\pi}\sum_{k_x}\Delta u_x e^{-\beta\hbar^2 k_x^2/2m}\\
&\simeq \frac{L}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}du_x\,e^{-\beta\hbar^2 k_x^2/2m}
\end{aligned}\tag{12}$$
