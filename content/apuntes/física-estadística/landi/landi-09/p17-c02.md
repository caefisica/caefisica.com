this discussion suggests interpreting $h^3$ as the box unit of volume in phase space : $d^3r d^3p \sim h^3$. this is corroborated by the Heisenberg uncertainty principle, which states that

$\Delta x \Delta p \sim h$ .

Many people like to correct this deficiency "by hand", redefining the classical partition function as

$$\begin{aligned}
Z_1^c &= \int e^{-\beta H}\frac{d^3r d^3p}{h^3}
\end{aligned}\tag{39}$$
