We are looking for a function $\psi$ such that $\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}$ is a constant  
times itself, the answer is, of course, the exponential  

$\psi(x) = e^{i\lambda x/\hbar}$  

Then $\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x} = \frac{i\lambda}{\hbar}e^{i\lambda x/\hbar} = \frac{i\lambda}{\hbar}\psi$
