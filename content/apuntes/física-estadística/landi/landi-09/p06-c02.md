Thus, we have just found the eigenfunctions of the operator $\hat{p}$.

It is convenient to write these eigenfunctions as

$$\psi_k(x) = e^{ikx}\tag{9}$$

Then

$$\hat{p}\psi_k(x) = -i\hbar \frac{\partial\psi_k}{\partial x} = -i\hbar (ik)e^{ikx} = \hbar k\psi_k(x)$$
