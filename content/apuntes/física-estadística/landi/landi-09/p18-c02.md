Note that the $k$'s only appear in the sum through the particular combination $E_k = \frac{\hbar^2}{2m}(k_x^2 + k_y^2 + k_z^2)$. The idea is to convert the sum over $(k_x,k_y,k_z)$ into an integral over $E$ [since the $k$ vary quasi-continuously, thus so will $E$].

That is, in general we want to approximate, for an arbitrary $f(E_k)$

$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k_x,k_y,k_z} f(E_k) &\simeq \int_0^\infty D(E)f(E)dE
\end{aligned}\tag{40}$$
