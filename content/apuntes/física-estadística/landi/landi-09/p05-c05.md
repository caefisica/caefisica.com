$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{p}\psi &= \lambda\psi
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$

$\lambda$ = eigenvalue of the momentum  
operator.

Explicitly eq (7) reads

$\hat{p}\psi = -i\hbar \frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x} = \lambda\psi$

or

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x} &= \frac{i\lambda}{\hbar}\psi
\end{aligned}\tag{8}$$
