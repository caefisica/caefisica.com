Schrödinger's Equation

The canonical way of formulating quantum mechanics  
is to promote observables to operators. The Hamiltonian of a  
particle in 1 D is

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{H} &= \frac{\hat{p}^{2}}{2m} + V(x)
\end{aligned}\tag{1}$$
