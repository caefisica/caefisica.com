Ideal gases with internal structure

The partition function of a gas of N particles is

$$\begin{aligned}
Z = Z_1^N = \frac{V^N}{(2\pi\hbar)^{3N}}(2\pi m k_B T)^{3N/2}
\end{aligned}\tag{43}$$

the internal energy is therefore

$$\begin{aligned}
U &= -\frac{\partial}{\partial\beta}\ln Z
= -\frac{3N}{2}\frac{\partial}{\partial\beta}\ln(1/\beta)
= -\frac{3N}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{\beta}\right)\\
\text{or}\qquad U &= \frac{3N}{2}k_B T
\end{aligned}\tag{44}$$

Thus the heat capacity is

$C = \frac{\partial U}{\partial T} = \frac{3N}{2}k_B$
