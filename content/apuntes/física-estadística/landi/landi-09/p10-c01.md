Now let us look at the energy of the particle in a box. The  
Hamiltonian is given in (3) with $V(x) = 0$. (this is a free particle,  
except for the box):

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{H} &= \frac{\hat{p}^2}{2m}
\end{aligned}\tag{19}$$

Now we want to find its eigenvalues and eigenfunctions

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{H}\psi &= E\psi
\end{aligned}\tag{20}$$
