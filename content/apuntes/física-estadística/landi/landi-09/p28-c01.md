Calculation of the rotational contribution to the  
specific heat

The energy from the rotations is

$$\begin{aligned}
E_\ell &= \frac{\hbar^2}{2I}\ell(\ell+1), \quad \ell=0,1,2,\ldots
\end{aligned}\tag{53}$$
