[operators wear hats, like $\hat{H}$, $\hat{p}_1$ etc.]. Since $\hat{p}$ and $\hat{H}$ are operators, they must now act on something. This "something" is called a wave function $\psi(x)$. Thus

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{p}\psi &= -i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x} = \text{some function}
\end{aligned}\tag{3}$$

Moreover

$$\hat{p}^2 = \left(-i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^2 = -\hbar^2\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}$$
