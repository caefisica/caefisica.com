Our result shows that the higher is the energy $E$, the larger  
is the number of allowed states.

The partition function may now be written as

$$\begin{aligned}
z_1 &= \int_0^\infty e^{-\beta E}D(E)\,dE.
\end{aligned}\tag{42}$$
