Extension to 3D

It is quite easy to extend all our previous calculations to  
3D. The Hamiltonian will now read

$$\begin{aligned}
\hat{H} = \frac{\hat{p}^{2}}{2m} = \frac{\hat{p}_{x}^{2}+\hat{p}_{y}^{2}+\hat{p}_{z}^{2}}{2m}
\end{aligned}\tag{29}$$
